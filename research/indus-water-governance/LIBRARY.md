## How to add an entry

Paste the link, add one line on why it matters, and mark access and read status.
Do not save a link without the "why" — in three months you will not remember,
and an unannotated link is only slightly better than no link.

If you have PDFs, keep them **outside this repo** (they are usually
copyright-restricted and this repo is headed for public). Store them in Drive or
Dropbox and reference the filename here.
