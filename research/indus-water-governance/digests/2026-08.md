## Scope change — Project 01

**The original Project 01 contribution is dead.** GRACE downscaling for the
Indus is published: Arshad et al. (2024) reach 1 km² across 20 sub-regions for
2002–2023. Building that pipeline would have been a reproduction exercise.

Project 01 has been rescoped to the institutional half — joining the published
depletion surface to institutional geography (canal position, tariff regime,
tenure class) and testing whether institutional variables explain residual
variance after climatic controls. This is a better project: it is the actual
seam between the two strands, and the expensive part is now an input rather
than something to build.

Cost of finding this now rather than in three months: one search. Cost of not
running it: three months of work someone had already published.
