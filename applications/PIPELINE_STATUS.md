## Tooling note — drafts

draft IDs without the draft appearing in the mailbox, and other calls failed
on an approval gate. **Verify with `list_drafts` before ever reporting that a
draft is waiting.** If it cannot be verified, hand the owner the text to paste
rather than claiming a draft exists. Drafts are only ever saved — never send
mail on the owner's behalf.
