# CIB Finance audit — background primer

General industry background, not JPMorgan-specific confidential detail. Useful
for framing answers, not for quoting as fact about this exact team.

## What CIB Finance audit typically covers

- **Regulatory capital and reporting**: Basel III risk-weighted assets (RWA),
  capital adequacy, CCAR/DFAST stress-testing submissions, FR Y-14 series,
  FFIEC 101, Pillar 3 disclosures
- **Balance sheet and liquidity controls**: funding, liquidity coverage ratio
  (LCR), net stable funding ratio (NSFR), resolution and recovery planning
- **Financial control environment**: month-end close, reconciliations,
  legal-entity reporting, management and regulatory reporting accuracy
- **Model risk touchpoints**: where Finance relies on models (capital
  forecasting, RWA calculation engines) audit assesses governance under
  SR 11-7

## What an audit interview at this level usually probes

- A specific example of finding a control gap and what you did about it
  (root cause, not just the symptom)
- How you handle disagreement with a business stakeholder about a finding's
  severity
- How you prioritize a risk-based audit plan when you can't test everything
- Whether you can translate a technical finance/regulatory issue into
  something a non-specialist can act on
- Evidence of stakeholder management across time zones/regions if the team
  is global (per the JD's preferred qualifications)
