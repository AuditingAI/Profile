   vector search over unstructured workpapers → SHAP/LIME so the output could be

   Built so the AI tool was itself auditable.
2. **Data blessing, from both sides.** Produced the certified data at Citi GLEM

   whether a population was complete enough to test.
3. **The statistics are formal.** MBA Financial Mathematics; IRB-25-0462 was a
   designed experiment analysed in SPSS, not a literature review.
4. **Direction, not just build.** Sample selection, control-gap identification,
   evidence that survives QA and an external regulator — five years of it across
   15+ business units.
