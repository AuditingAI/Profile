## How the letter handles it

It opens with the gaps rather than burying them: no product-management title, no
Snowflake, no HR. Then it makes the narrow argument — the hard half of a data
product in a regulated firm is lineage, quality, and defensibility, and that is
exactly the GLEM programme. It closes by naming the ramp honestly (a quarter on
the HCM domain; useful on governance from week one, credible on Snowflake in a
few months) and by saying plainly which shape of candidate he is not.

That structure is deliberate. A screener who reads the first paragraph and stops
has lost nothing he would not have found anyway; one who keeps reading gets the
real argument.
