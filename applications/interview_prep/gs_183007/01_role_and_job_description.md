## Team

> The Supervisory Risk & Controls team within Global Banking & Markets (GBM)
> manages controls covering supervisory-related risks arising from business
> activities in the GBM division. The team partners with businesses to
> implement controls, support ongoing monitoring and oversight, as well as
> related supervisory tools to manage risk.

> We are looking for an individual to drive the expansion of the program to
> cover business activities in **GBM Private**, focusing on the **Capital
> Solutions Group (CSG)**.

CSG is Goldman's financing / private-credit / structured-solutions business.
"GBM Private" = the private-side (non-public-markets) businesses. The role is
about extending a supervisory-controls program that already exists on the
public side into CSG.

## Responsibilities (verbatim)

- Develop understanding of Capital Solutions Group (CSG) business activities
- Develop the supervisory framework for CSG business activities, including
  appropriate desk controls, monitoring & tools
- Define appropriate controls to manage risk, including supervisor expectations
  and escalation
- Define governance processes to drive monitoring & oversight for the covered
  controls
- Build relationships with relevant stakeholder groups to implement the program,
  including bankers, Compliance, Engineering, Legal and others, as relevant
- Represent the team in relevant governance forums to demonstrate 1st line
  monitoring & engagement with business teams
- Engage with GBM Public side teams to drive consistency across the divisional
  supervisory program and related monitoring

## Qualifications (verbatim, as cached)

- 8–10 years of relevant work experience with specific knowledge of supervisory
  frameworks or exposure to regulatory implementation.
