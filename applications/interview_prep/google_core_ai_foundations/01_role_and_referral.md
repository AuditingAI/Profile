## The honest read

This is the largest stretch in the pipeline, and it was argued before it was
built. Two of the stated minimums are hard, checkable facts that the record
does not meet:

- **Product-manager years: zero.** No PM title anywhere on the record. The
  closest real artefact is the AI tooling roadmap for Citi Internal Audit —
  genuine roadmap ownership, but inside an audit function, not a product org.
- **Degree: B.Sc. Banking & Finance (LSE), not Computer Science.**

A Google VP PM over the core model stack is normally someone who has run large
product organisations through several model generations. A résumé alone will
almost certainly not clear the screen.

**The argument that is real, and narrow:** he holds the trust surface of an AI
platform — governance framework authored against NIST AI RMF and SR 11-7, risk
tiering, explainability and human-in-the-loop requirements, post-deployment
monitoring KPIs — *and* he shipped the retrieval system that framework governed,
in Python and LangChain, to production. Governance people who cannot build write
policy nobody implements; builders without governance ship what cannot be
defended. Doing both is rare. Add the bank-examiner background and doctoral
research on automation bias, and there is a coherent case for a **trust /
responsible-AI leadership seat** — which is what the cover letter asks for
explicitly, including below the posted level.

**Do not claim:** a product-manager title, a CS degree, having run a PM
organisation, or P&L ownership of a product line.
