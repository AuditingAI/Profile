# The positioning story: one career, six doors

*Industry lane. Last updated 30 Sep 2026. This is the map every job-list email follows.*

## The spine: what we are carving

Most candidates in this market come from one seat. Auditors audit AI, risk managers score it,
technologists build it. Yasir's record covers all four seats around the same control system, so
the new category is:

> **The person who has examined, run, audited and built the control system, and is now rebuilding
> it for AI.**

The executive résumé states it in one line: **"Inside Two Rebuilds of the Control System: After
2008, and Now for AI."**

**The rebuild frame is the pitch; each résumé below is the same story told from one door.**

## What never moves, on any door

These rules come from `agent-kit/rules.json`.

- **Same titles and dates on every résumé.** JPM titles are Program Manager, Capital Controller and Risk Control Manager.
- **Never stated or claimed:**
  - no career-length number;
  - no DBA completion year or GPA;
  - no "OCC";
  - no claimed certifications.
- **Business-impact voice, never academic voice.** The research appears as a credential ("doctoral
  research on AI-assisted audit judgment"), never as findings.
- **One résumé per application.** It never goes out beside the academic CV.

## How every job email works from now on

1. Roles come from his LinkedIn alerts, then Indeed. Each role carries its ID and has been checked
   against everything he has applied to.
2. **The roles are grouped under the six doors, in the order above.** Each door carries its résumé
   link and, for JPMorgan, its cover.
3. The email ends with the removed roles and the reason each was removed.

## What we learn and change

- **Watch which door gets replies.** Each week, the dashboard's interviews and rejections are counted per door.
  - A door with replies gets more roles.
  - A door that gets none for three weeks gets its résumé reworked, or it is closed.
- **Gaps to fill next:**
  - **Doors 1 and 2 have no non-JPM cover letter.** Build one employer-neutral cover per door, with a single line to name the employer.
  - **The executive résumé should carry an AI-assurance line near the top** for Head-of-AI-Governance postings (Guardian, MUFG, State Street).
