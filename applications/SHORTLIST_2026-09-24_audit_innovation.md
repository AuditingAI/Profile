## The angle that is new, and worth leading with

**SR 26-2** (17 April 2026) superseded SR 11-7 as the interagency model risk
guidance — and it puts **generative and agentic AI expressly outside its
scope**, with AI-specific guidance promised later. Every firm on this list has
GenAI in production and no supervisory framework covering it. That gap is the
argument: he has already written a control framework for exactly those systems,
and he can point at the regulator's own words for why one was needed.

Use it in the first line of any cover letter or recruiter reply here.
