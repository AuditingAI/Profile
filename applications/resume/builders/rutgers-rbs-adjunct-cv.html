Doctoral Research

Dissertation (in development): Anchoring Bias in LLM-Assisted Audit Judgment. A mixed-methods programme asking how professional judgment shifts when a large language model supplies a confident starting point — and what an auditor must test to demonstrate independent judgment was exercised.

  • Quantitative arm. A 55-item instrument on anchoring in audit judgment, fielded under IRB-25-0462. Recruitment of a rare professional population — practising auditors — proved the binding methodological constraint, and the recruitment design is itself documented as a contribution.
