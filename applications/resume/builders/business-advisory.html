The Problem I Solve

AI-assisted review can pass every model validation and still degrade the judgment it was meant to support. The model checks out; the people using it quietly stop checking. Most firms have governance for the first failure and nothing for the second — and since April 2026 the regulator’s own model risk guidance, SR 26-2, puts generative and agentic AI expressly outside its scope. The control expectations for those systems are not yet written. I help firms write theirs before an examiner asks, from the perspective of someone who has been the examiner, the first line, and the auditor.
