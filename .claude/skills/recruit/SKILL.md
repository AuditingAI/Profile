# Recruit — daily push to n = 100

The study cannot be completed without the sample. This is the only workstream where the number
going up is the whole point.

## Step 0 — orient

Read `dba/00_Execution/RECRUITMENT_PLAN_LinkedIn.md` (targeting, message drafts, the arithmetic) and
`dba/00_Execution/RECRUITMENT_TRACKER.md` (where the funnel stands). Nothing else.

## Standing facts

- **Cleared to run.** LinkedIn recruitment is covered by IRB-25-0462; the approval is not filed in
  this repo, so it rests on Yasir's statement.
- **Population is auditors AND risk governance professionals** — risk, compliance, controls, model
  validation, AI governance. Not auditors alone. This widening is recent; do not narrow it back.
- **20 connection requests per day, maximum.** No automation. A restricted account ends the channel.
- **First-degree contacts convert ~8× better than cold.** Always push that channel first.

## The daily output

When he says he is doing outreach, produce exactly this and stop:

1. **One title to search today**, with the filter string, rotating through the target list
2. **20 personalised connection notes** — under 300 characters each, no link
3. **Follow-up messages** for anyone who accepted since last time
4. **The tracker line to append**

Nothing else. He is doing 45 minutes of repetitive work; the output should be paste-ready.

## When he asks where it stands

Read the tracker and report four numbers: **valid responses · this week's adds · the gap to 100 ·
weeks remaining at the current rate.** Then one line on which channel is underperforming.

No charts unless asked. No encouragement. The number is the message.

## Message rules

- Connection request: **never** contains the survey link
- Follow-up: says what it is, how long it takes, that it is anonymous and IRB approved, and gives an
  easy way to decline
- First-degree: **always** asks for the forward — that sentence is what multiplies the channel
- Every message is a draft. He sends. Academic mail goes from `ymali001@fiu.edu`

## Log discipline

Every session appends to the tracker: date · channel · title searched · requests · accepts ·
follow-ups · starts · completes · **screen-outs and the criterion each failed on**.

The screen-out reasons matter as much as the completes. If failures cluster on one criterion, that
criterion is too tight, and knowing which is a finding.

## Guardrails

- Never fabricate a response count, a rate, or a contact
- Never draft anything implying employer endorsement or obligation to participate
- Never suggest an automation tool
- If he is short of time, cut the cold batch and keep the first-degree asks
