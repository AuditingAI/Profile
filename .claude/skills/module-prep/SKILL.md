# Module prep — GEB 7365

## The assignment, in the professor's terms

- **Three or four articles**, emailed by the professor for the assigned week
- **Write-up: up to 10 pages, double-spaced**
- **Presentation: up to 10 minutes**
- Usually **two students per week**

**The split rule, verbatim:** *"It's fine to do that for the presentation, but for the write-up, you
should still analyze all four."* You divide the talking. You each write everything.

## The four required components — structure by these, never by article

1. **Major findings of the research area**
2. **Its importance and contribution to IB**
3. **Its limitations**
4. **Connections to other course topics and to prior coursework**

A write-up organised article-by-article reads as a summary. Organised by these four, it reads as an
analysis. That difference is the grade.

## Where the marks are

**Section 3 is where a good write-up separates itself.** Every literature has a structural weakness —
usually measurement, sampling, or an untested assumption everyone inherited. Find the one this
literature actually has, not a generic caveat.

**Section 4 is where his own work enters.** Connect forward to coming sessions, back to residency 1,
and across to the dissertation. Newburry is an author on several assigned papers — where a
connection runs through his own work, say so.

## Build the comparison table

His words: *"Some people put a table to compare the articles."* Four columns: **claim · method ·
sample · what it conditions on**. It is the fastest route to seeing where the papers actually
disagree, and it belongs in the write-up.

## Presenting

Split by **argument**, not page count — both halves are then easier to deliver and neither person
is narrating. He said he does not want the articles re-narrated: the class discusses them, the
presentation frames them.

Ten minutes **total**, not each.

## Output

A skeleton with the four components as headings, the reading order with one question per paper, the
comparison table stub, and a proposed split to send the partner. Deliverables are Word — build with
`dba/coursework/_templates/build_submission.py`.

## Guardrails

- The professor's email governs the article list. If it differs from the syllabus, his email wins
- **Check the AI policy for this course** before drafting anything graded — it was added to the
  syllabus after distribution and is not the same as GEB 7911's
- Fields he must supply go ALL CAPS on a yellow highlight, inline
