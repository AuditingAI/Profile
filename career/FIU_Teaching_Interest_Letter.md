**Purpose:** open the teaching conversation while the relationship is warm (he just passed the qualifying exam and wrote a congratulatory close), ask for his guidance on the right path and person, and put the CV in his hands.

## Why this version works

- **Gratitude first, and specific** — quotes back the thing he actually praised, which shows you read his feedback rather than skimmed the grade.
- **Separates the ask** — "I wanted to raise something separate" signals that the thanks wasn't a setup for a favor.
- **Asks for guidance, not a job** — the easiest thing for a professor to say yes to, and it routes you to the actual decision-maker (likely the School of Accounting chair, or Miguel Aguirre-Urreta as GPD).
- **Names the courses implicitly** through your background rather than listing them — avoids sounding like you're auditioning.
- **Removes pressure** — "no urgency" makes a reply more likely, not less.
