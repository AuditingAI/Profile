cross-border AI (data privacy, ethical dilemmas, job displacement, regulatory divergence) and the
opportunity case; cultural insights by region (Latin America, Asia-Pacific, Middle East, Africa);
opportunities in customer engagement, operations, product innovation and market expansion; live demo;
five forces reshaping global business (digital economy, Industry 4.0, climate, water scarcity,
demographics), presented by Yasir.

## LinkedIn post, draft for Yasir to rewrite in his own voice

> Last September Dr. Sumit Kundu invited me back to FIU to guest-lecture his International MBA class
> on AI, culture and the political economy, alongside my DBA colleague Eustache Dupervil.
>
> The question we put to the room: does "trustworthy AI" mean the same thing in São Paulo, Singapore,
> Dubai and Lagos? It does not. Transparency reads as openness in one market and as control in
> another, and an AI system that ignores that difference will be adopted in one place and quietly
> worked around in the next.
>
> We closed with a live demo instead of a summary slide, and the best part of the session was the
> class arguing about what the model got wrong.
>
> Grateful to Dr. Kundu for the invitation, and to the students for the pushback. This is the
