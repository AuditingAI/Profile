I am applying to teach your online graduate course in Accounting Information Systems. The course description reads like a summary of my working life: transaction cycles, stakeholders' information needs, internal controls and data governance, and enterprise integration.

At Citi I began in the Office of the Chief Accounting Officer, bringing legal-entity data from many source systems into governed master data that fed U.S. regulatory filings. At JPMorgan Chase I worked further down the same pipeline, on Basel III capital and risk reporting such as the FR Y-14Q and FFIEC 101, where a control weakness upstream becomes a reporting error downstream. I returned to Citi as a Vice President in Internal Audit, testing controls across more than fifteen business units and reporting to the Board Audit Committee. Before banking, I examined state-chartered banks for the Florida Office of Financial Regulation alongside the FDIC and the Federal Reserve Bank of Atlanta. I have worked on the management, regulator and auditor side of the systems your students will learn to design.

Emerging technology is where I can add most. At Citi I designed and deployed a retrieval-augmented AI assistant that cut audit review time by roughly 35 percent, and wrote the governance framework to control it, grounded in the NIST AI Risk Management Framework and federal model risk guidance, now SR 26-2. My doctoral research at Florida International University, where I passed the qualifying examination in July 2026, studies how an auditor's judgment shifts when an AI system offers a conclusion that agrees with their own. Students entering practice need to document, control and audit systems like that, and I would build it into cases and assessments aligned with the CPA exam's Information Systems and Controls content.

I would build my section in Canvas as the posting describes: revising the syllabus, vetting the textbook, recording the lectures, and designing assessments around control failures drawn, anonymized, from real engagements. My university teaching began at FIU, as a lab assistant in the Capital Markets Lab and a tutor in corporate finance and accounting, and in September 2025 I guest-lectured FIU's International MBA class on AI and international business at the invitation of Dr. Sumit K. Kundu. Much of my teaching since has been to professionals, training auditors in testing methodology, workpaper review and responsible AI use.

I hold an MBA from FIU and a B.Sc. (Honours) in Banking and Finance from the London School of Economics. Professor William Newburry and Dr. Juan C. Rey of FIU have agreed to serve as references. I live in Newark and can begin this term or next.
