## The email

**To:** Dr. Himanshu Upadhyay; Dr. Alexander Perez-Pons
**Subject:** A responsible AI course for the MS in Applied AI & IoT

Dear Dr. Upadhyay and Dr. Perez-Pons,

I am a doctoral candidate in FIU's DBA program, and my research looks at how AI changes professional judgment
when people review its output. I am writing to propose a course for the MS in Applied AI & IoT: Responsible AI
Engineering, on the ethics, safety and governance of the systems your students learn to build.

Your curriculum already teaches students to build machine learning, deep learning, generative and embedded AI.
I could not find a course dedicated to whether and how those systems should be deployed, and to the people they
affect. That question is now part of the job. The NIST AI Risk Management Framework, ISO/IEC 42001 and the EU AI
Act all expect engineers to document, test and justify what they build. I believe FIU can keep ethical AI at the
front of the program, for the good of the communities our graduates will serve.

The course would turn the responsible AI principles students already meet in AI-900 into engineering work. Each
student would leave with a responsible AI dossier for a system they built in the program: a model card, a
datasheet, a bias test, a privacy design and an impact assessment.

I would bring the governance and audit side of this work. At Citi I designed a retrieval-augmented review
assistant for auditors and wrote the governance proposal that went with it. Earlier I was a bank examiner with the
Florida Office of Financial Regulation. I passed my qualifying examination in July 2026, and a paper of mine has
been accepted for presentation at the AIB Latin America and Caribbean Chapter conference in 2027.

I have attached a two-page outline and my CV. The course could run as an elective, perhaps first as a section of
EEL 5799, as a module inside existing courses, or as a workshop series. I would be glad to teach it, develop it
with your faculty, or support a course you already offer. Could we meet for twenty minutes, at the Engineering
Center or on Zoom, in the coming weeks?

Thank you for considering it.

With best regards,
