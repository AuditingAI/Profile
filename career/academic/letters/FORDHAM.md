# Fordham University — Letter of Interest

## Targeting

**Role types to aim at.** Adjunct or part-time teaching in the Accounting and Taxation area of the
Gabelli School of Business, and lecturer or professor-of-practice lines if one opens. Fordham teaches
business at both the Rose Hill and Lincoln Center campuses; Lincoln Center is the more practical
commute from Newark and the more likely home for evening sections, but do not assert in the letter
which campus a given course runs on.

**Why this institution.** Fordham is a private Jesuit university and its business teaching is
explicitly formation-oriented — ethics and professional responsibility are curricular commitments
rather than a single required course. That is an unusually good match for a candidate whose research
subject *is* the integrity of professional judgment under pressure, and it is the one place among
these four where the research question and the institutional mission argue for each other. Lead on
that connection here and nowhere else.

**Where their postings appear.** Fordham's careers portal. Area-level adjunct hiring in business
schools is frequently handled by the area or department directly, so a letter to the chair is a real
channel alongside the portal.

**What is not known.** The area chair's name, whether an adjunct pool is maintained, current course
coverage gaps, and campus assignment. Do not invent any of these, and do not name a Fordham course
number, centre, or institute.

## Notes

**Insert the actual area chair's name.** Confirm it on Gabelli's own faculty listing. If the area
uses an area head rather than a department chair, address that person by their actual title.

**Cold inquiry vs. specific posting.** Written as a cold inquiry. Against a live posting, replace the
first paragraph with the posting title and number, state that the portal application has been filed,
and cut the second paragraph to two sentences — a posting response should not spend its first third
on institutional fit.

**The mission paragraph is deliberate and is unique to this letter.** Do not port it into the Baruch,
St. John's, or FIU letters; the same paragraph appearing at four schools is what makes a letter read
as a template.

**Attach:** academic CV, teaching statement (once written), research statement. The research
statement earns its place here more than at the other three.

**Do not include:** DBA completion year, GPA, publications, teaching evaluations, or memberships.
