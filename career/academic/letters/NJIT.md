# New Jersey Institute of Technology, Martin Tuchman School of Management — Letter of Interest

**Targeting**

- **Roles to aim at:** professor of practice / professional practice, university lecturer, or
  senior university lecturer in the Martin Tuchman School of Management, with adjunct as the parallel
  route. NJIT is the one target in this set where the AI governance material is the *lead* credential
  rather than a differentiator appended to an accounting profile.
- **Why this institution specifically:** NJIT is a public technological research university, and its
  management school teaches business to a heavily technical student body. That inverts the usual
  pitch. Elsewhere he is an accounting teacher who happens to have built an AI system; here he is
  someone who shipped a retrieval-augmented system into a regulated production environment and can
  teach students who can already build the model why validating each component does not make the
  system safe. Accounting information systems, analytics, internal controls and AI governance are the
  courses to foreground; behavioural accounting and international financial markets go in the
  secondary list.
- **Where postings appear:** NJIT posts faculty and adjunct openings through its own university
  careers site; management-school lines also appear on HigherEdJobs, and accounting-specific lines
  may appear on the AAA Career Center. **Confirm the current portal before applying.**
- **Known department detail:** the school is the Martin Tuchman School of Management. **Unlike the
  other three targets, I have no verified information that it contains a separate accounting
  department** — it appears to operate as a single management school rather than as departmentalised
  units, so the letter is addressed to the school and avoids naming a department. Its programs, course
  catalogue, teaching loads, centres, and current leadership are **not verified** and must not be
  asserted. Check the school's own site and address the letter to the correct office before sending.

## Notes

**Insert the actual name.** Replace "Dear Professor —," with the verified recipient — dean, associate
dean for faculty, or the relevant program director, depending on how the school is organised. Because
the internal structure is unverified, confirm who handles faculty hiring before sending rather than
guessing at a department chair who may not exist.

**Applying to a specific posting vs. a cold inquiry**

- *Specific posting:* name the posting and requisition number in the opening sentence, and replace
  the course list in paragraph two with the courses the posting names. If the posting is in
  information systems or analytics rather than accounting, move the AI-governance paragraph and the
  Citi system ahead of the accounting course list, and compress the auditing material — at NJIT that
  reordering is likely, not exceptional.
- *Cold inquiry:* keep as written. This is the strongest cold letter of the four, because the pitch
  is specific to what NJIT is rather than to what any business school is. If it needs trimming, cut
  the FIU list to the two strongest appointments rather than shortening the AI-governance paragraph.
