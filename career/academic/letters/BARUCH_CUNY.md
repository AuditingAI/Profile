# Baruch College, CUNY — Letter of Interest

## Targeting

**Role types to aim at.** Adjunct teaching in the Stan Ross Department of Accountancy first, and
lecturer or clinical lines if and when they open. CUNY's teaching titles differ from the private-school
vocabulary — do not name a specific CUNY title in the letter unless it appears verbatim in a posting.
"Adjunct or part-time teaching" is accurate and safe.

**Why this institution.** Baruch runs one of the largest accounting programmes in the country and a
heavily CPA-track student body, much of it working and commuting. That produces two things Yasir is
well-matched to: a persistent need for reliable section coverage in auditing, AIS and internal
controls, and students who are already working in finance and respond to an instructor who has done
the work rather than only read about it. It is also the only one of the four schools reachable from
Newark on a single transit ride.

**Where their postings appear.** CUNY's central careers site and the Baruch HR pages. Departmental
adjunct hiring at CUNY is frequently done by the department directly, off-cycle, when a section needs
covering — so a cold letter to the chair is a genuine channel here, not just a formality.

**What is not known.** The current chair's name, whether the department maintains a standing adjunct
pool, and which courses are short-staffed for any given term. Do not guess any of these. No specific
Baruch course numbers should appear in the letter.

## Notes

**Insert the actual chair's name** in place of "Dear Professor —" before sending. Verify it on the
department's own faculty page, not a third-party directory, and check the spelling and the preferred
form of address.

**Cold inquiry vs. specific posting.** As written this is a cold inquiry. If a requisition is open,
change the first sentence to name the posting title and number, move the availability sentence to the
close, and add one sentence stating that the application has also been filed through the CUNY portal
— departments cannot hire off a letter alone.

**Attach:** academic CV, teaching statement (once written), research statement. Do not attach the
qualifying manuscript unless asked; offer it instead.

**Do not include:** any DBA completion year, any GPA, any claim of publications, teaching
evaluations, or professional memberships. There are none, and the completion year and GPA are both
unresolved.

**Contact fields** are `{{PHONE}}` and `{{EMAIL}}` placeholders because this repository is public.
Fill them at send time.
