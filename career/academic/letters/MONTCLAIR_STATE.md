# Montclair State University, Feliciano School of Business — Letter of Interest

**Targeting**

- **Roles to aim at:** lecturer / instructor and adjunct first, professor of practice second, in the
  Department of Accounting and Finance. Montclair is a large regional public university whose
  business school carries substantial undergraduate teaching, and lecturer and adjunct lines there
  are the most realistic entry point in the whole New Jersey set. Adjuncting also produces the one
  thing the file is missing entirely: a teaching record and teaching evaluations.
- **Why this institution specifically:** the fit here is teaching volume and course breadth, not
  research. He can cover auditing, AIS, internal controls, ERM and international financial markets,
  which means a department can hand him whatever section is short rather than hiring narrowly. The
  letter should therefore lead on *coverage and availability*, and should not oversell the
  dissertation — at a teaching-focused regional public, a candidate who sounds like he is waiting to
  become a researcher reads as a flight risk.
- **Where postings appear:** Montclair posts faculty and adjunct openings through its own university
  careers site, and business-school lines are also commonly listed on HigherEdJobs and the AAA Career
  Center. Adjunct pools at regional publics are frequently a standing, rolling posting rather than a
  dated vacancy. **Confirm the current portal and whether an adjunct pool is open before applying.**
- **Known department detail:** the school is named the Feliciano School of Business and the relevant
  unit is the Department of Accounting and Finance. **I do not have verified information** about its
  current course catalogue, section counts, teaching loads, standing coverage gaps, accreditation
  reporting posture, or chair. None of that should be asserted in the letter.

## Notes

**Insert the actual name.** Replace "Dear Professor —," with the current chair of the Department of
Accounting and Finance, verified on the department's own page. If the letter is going into an adjunct
pool rather than to a person, address it to the department by name rather than inventing a recipient.

**Applying to a specific posting vs. a cold inquiry**

- *Specific posting:* name the posting and requisition number in the first sentence, and replace the
  general course list in paragraph two with the specific courses the posting names, using the
  posting's wording. If the posting is for an adjunct pool, state the terms and days he is available
  — regional publics staff pools on availability as much as on credentials.
- *Cold inquiry:* keep as written. This letter is deliberately the most availability-forward of the
  four; that is the right register for a cold approach to a teaching-heavy regional public. If it
  needs to be shorter, cut the research paragraph to one sentence rather than cutting the teaching
  history.

**Attach**

- Academic CV (`../ACADEMIC_CV_v2.md`), after the `[VERIFY]` items are resolved.
- Teaching statement — **not written yet.** Montclair postings are the most likely of the four to
  require it outright. Write it before applying.
- Research statement only if the posting asks for one; for an adjunct approach it is unnecessary and
  can work against him.

**Do not**

- State a DBA completion year or any GPA.
- Call the manuscript under review or forthcoming — it is in preparation, and he has no publications.
- Imply he has held a faculty appointment; the FIU roles were graduate appointments and are described
  as such.
