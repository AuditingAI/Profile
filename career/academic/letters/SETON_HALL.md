  Accounting and Taxation, with adjunct as the parallel low-barrier route. Seton Hall is a private
  university with a smaller business school than Rutgers or Montclair, which usually means smaller
  sections, closer advising, and hiring decisions made by fewer people — a setting where a warm

  years — workpaper review, methodology instruction, structured feedback — and then sat on the

  school is most likely to want taught well. Say that plainly; do not perform piety about it.
- **Where postings appear:** Seton Hall runs faculty and adjunct hiring through its own university
  employment site, and business lines are also typically listed on HigherEdJobs and the AAA Career
  Center. **Confirm the current portal before applying.**
- **Known department detail:** the business school is the Stillman School of Business and the unit is
