# Rutgers Business School – Newark — Letter of Interest

**Targeting**

- **Roles to aim at:** professor of practice / professional practice, clinical faculty, or lecturer in
  the Department of Accounting and Information Systems. Do **not** aim at tenure-track assistant
  professor here — Rutgers Newark is a doctoral-granting, research-active public university, and a
  tenure-track line will want Scholarly Academic status with a publication record. That is not this
  cycle (see `../ACADEMIC_JOB_STRATEGY.md` §2).
- **Why this institution specifically:** it is the closest research-active business school to Newark
  07107 — he can be on campus without relocating or committing to a long commute. The department
  pairs accounting with information systems in a single unit, which is the exact seam his work sits
  on: auditor judgment on one side, AI-assisted review systems on the other. Of the four NJ targets,
  this is the one where the research paragraph does real work rather than sitting politely at the end.
- **Where postings appear:** Rutgers runs university-wide hiring through its central jobs portal
  (jobs.rutgers.edu), and business-school faculty lines are also typically listed on the AAA Career
  Center and HigherEdJobs. **Verify the current portal URL and posting location before applying** —
  do not cite a posting in the letter that has not been confirmed.
- **Known department detail:** the department's name establishes the accounting/AIS pairing, and the
  school operates on both the Newark and New Brunswick campuses. **Beyond that, specifics are not
  verified** — course numbers, named centres, program structures, current chair, and standing
  teaching needs are all unknown here and must not be asserted. `ACADEMIC_JOB_STRATEGY.md` §5 notes
  a possible prior Rutgers Newark adjunct application; **that status is unresolved** and should be
  checked before contact, since a prior application may already be on file.

## Notes

**Insert the actual name.** Replace "Dear Professor —," with the current department chair's name and
correct form of address before sending. Verify it on the department's own faculty page — not from
memory, and not from a search result that may be out of date.

**Applying to a specific posting vs. a cold inquiry**

- *Specific posting:* change the first sentence to name the posting title and requisition number, and
  drop "expects to have… open." Add one sentence early on naming the specific courses the posting
  lists, drawn from the posting's own language. Move the practitioner paragraph up if the posting
  emphasises industry experience; leave it where it is if the posting emphasises teaching load.
- *Cold inquiry:* keep as written, and consider trimming the research paragraph by a sentence — a
  cold letter that is too long does not get read. Also resolve the prior-adjunct-application question
  first; opening a cold inquiry when an application is already on file looks disorganised.

**Attach**

- Academic CV (`../ACADEMIC_CV_v2.md`) — **only after the `[VERIFY]` items are resolved.**
- Research statement (`../RESEARCH_STATEMENT.md`) — rewritten in his own voice first.
- Teaching statement — **does not exist yet and is required by most postings.** This letter cannot go
  to a formal posting until it does.
- Writing sample: the qualifying manuscript, if requested.

**Do not**

- State a DBA completion year or any GPA. Both are unresolved and both are verified by committees.
- Describe the manuscript as under review, forthcoming, or published. It is in preparation.
- Send the industry resume with this, or in any thread that touches this.
