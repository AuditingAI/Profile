# St. John's University — Letter of Interest

## Targeting

**Role type.** This one is not a cold inquiry. St. John's currently has a posted **Fall 2026
Accountancy Adjunct Instructor** line in the Department of Accounting and Taxation, Peter J. Tobin
College of Business, with compensation stated at **$7,500–$11,000 per course depending on rank**. The
letter below is written as a response to that posting and should not be reused as a general inquiry
without rewriting the opening.

**Why this institution.** A posted, funded, per-course adjunct line is the single most winnable thing
on the academic track right now — it is the appointment type the strategy document identifies as the
realistic entry point, and it is the appointment type that produces the teaching evaluations that
everything else is currently blocked on. Tobin's accounting programme is CPA-oriented and the adjunct
faculty is drawn substantially from practice, which is the pool Yasir belongs to.

**Where their postings appear.** The St. John's careers portal. The application must go through the
portal; the letter to the chair supplements it and does not replace it.

**What is not known.** Which campus and which courses the line covers, whether it is one section or
several, the rank criteria behind the salary band, and the chair's name. Do not guess. Do not name a
St. John's course number.

## Notes

**Insert the actual chair's name.** If the posting names a search contact instead, address that
person and say which posting you are responding to in the first line.

**File the portal application first,** then send this. The sentence claiming the application is
already submitted must be true when the letter arrives.

**Do not raise compensation.** The band is posted; rank is the department's determination, not a
negotiation to open in a first letter.

**If this posting closes,** the letter converts to a cold inquiry by replacing the first paragraph
with a request to be considered for future adjunct terms and to be kept in any standing pool, and by
removing the "full Fall 2026 term" specificity.

**Attach:** academic CV, teaching statement (once written). Add the research statement only if the
posting asks for it — an adjunct application that leads with research reads as someone applying to
the wrong line.

**Do not include:** DBA completion year, GPA, publications, teaching evaluations, or memberships.
None of those can be asserted.
