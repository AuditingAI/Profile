## Research

### Qualifying study (completed and passed, July 2026)

***Mitigating Anchoring Bias in Long-Term Auditor Engagements.*** An eleven-construct, fifty-five-item
measurement model with sixteen hypotheses, grounded in dual-process theory and the
anchoring-and-adjustment heuristic. Eight organisational interventions operating through two
mediating mechanisms — audit judgment quality and audit process rigour — toward one outcome:
judgments driven by current evidence rather than the prior-period figure.

IRB-approved (IRB-25-0462). Instrument developed, pilot-tested, and fielded. Complete analytic
protocol specified from exploratory factor analysis through confirmatory factor analysis, regression
diagnostics, and structural modelling. Manuscript ~16,000 words.

**Outcome and contribution.** The study reached feasibility rather than validation. Applying the
eligibility criteria to a commercial research panel of 334,976 participants returned approximately
twenty eligible individuals — a prevalence near six per hundred thousand. Compensation and
instrument design were both ruled out: effective compensation ran well above typical academic-survey
rates, attention-check failures among eligible respondents were zero, and every usable respondent
produced substantive open-text material. The constraint was arithmetic, and it was observable on the
platform's configuration screen before funds were committed.

Faculty assessment: *"Rather than simply acknowledging the limitation, you developed well-supported
recommendations for future research… This type of critical self-evaluation demonstrates sound
research judgment."* — Dr. Juan C. Rey, FIU College of Business.

### Research instruments and open materials

**Feasibility calculator.** The argument in the research note above, built as a working instrument
rather than left as an anecdote. Reachable usable sample is modelled as a product of frame size,
eligible prevalence, response rate and screen survival. It is validated by reproducing the study that
produced it, and it shows that a survey at this prevalence would have needed a sampling frame near

**Model-agnostic research runbooks.** A capture schema with provenance on every row, a rule that a

replaceable.

### Research programme in development

The dissertation extends the validated human-side model into AI-assisted review through a three-link
causal chain: system output becomes the anchor, arriving before the reviewer forms an independent
view; the model confirms rather than challenges the position already stated; and successive models
reprocessing the same work converge on one another rather than on evidence. The governance
consequence is that recurring professional judgment can degrade while every individual model passes
its own validation.

The empirical contribution targets the weakest link — whether professionals reading model output
converge on each other — which is measurable and, to date, unmeasured.

The chain is argued link by link rather than asserted, and each link carries its strongest challenge
in writing: the first must be argued from expert-population evidence rather than undergraduate lab
samples; the second describes current model behaviour and so is argued as a mechanism, the pressure
to optimise on human approval, rather than as a property of any one model generation; and the third
is stated plainly as an inference rather than a finding. Nothing in the chain has been tested, and
the programme says so wherever it is presented.
