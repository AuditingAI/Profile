Research programme in development
The dissertation extends the validated human-side model into AI-assisted review through a three-link causal chain: system output
becomes the anchor, arriving before the reviewer forms an independent view; the model confirms rather than challenges the position
already stated; and successive models reprocessing the same work converge on one another rather than on evidence. The governance
consequence is that recurring professional judgment can degrade while every individual model passes its own validation.
The empirical contribution targets the weakest link — whether professionals reading model output converge on each other — which
is measurable and, to date, unmeasured.
The chain is argued link by link rather than asserted, and each link carries its strongest challenge in writing: the first must be argued
from expert-population evidence rather than undergraduate lab samples; the second describes current model behaviour and so is
argued as a mechanism, the pressure to optimise on human approval, rather than as a property of any one model generation; and the
third is stated plainly as an inference rather than a finding. Nothing in the chain has been tested, and the programme says so
wherever it is presented.
