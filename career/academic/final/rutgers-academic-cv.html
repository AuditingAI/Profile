Research

Qualifying study: Mitigating Anchoring Bias in Long-Term Auditor Engagements2026
  • 55-item measurement model, 11 constructs, 16 hypotheses; instrument designed, pilot-tested and fielded under IRB approval (IRB-25-0462).
  • Key finding: a research panel of 334,976 held about 20 eligible auditors (roughly 6 per 100,000), showing that specialist professionals cannot be sampled from general panels.
  • Contribution: a pre-fielding prevalence check, and a feasibility calculator (Python, public) that models reachable sample from frame size, eligible prevalence, response rate and screen survival.
Dissertation in development: Anchoring Bias in LLM-Assisted Audit Judgment2026 –
  • Extends the human-side model to AI-assisted review: the model's output becomes the anchor, confirms rather than challenges the reviewer, and successive reviews converge on each other rather than on the evidence.
  • A phenomenological protocol for a qualitative arm is designed; nothing has been fielded.
