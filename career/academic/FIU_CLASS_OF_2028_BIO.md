**Yasir A. Malik**
*Audit, Risk & AI Governance Leader | Doctoral Candidate, Class of 2028*

Yasir A. Malik is an audit, risk and AI governance leader whose career has spanned both sides of financial regulation. Yasir began as a bank examiner with the Florida Office of Financial Regulation, examining state-chartered banks alongside the FDIC and the Federal Reserve Bank of Atlanta. He went on to Citi's Office of the Chief Accounting Officer, then to JPMorgan Chase, where he led capital reporting, first-line controls and recovery-planning work, and returned to Citi as Vice President and Audit Manager in Internal Audit, reporting to the Board Audit Committee. At Citi, Yasir built a retrieval-augmented AI assistant that cut audit review time by roughly 35 percent, and proposed an AI governance framework grounded in the NIST AI Risk Management Framework and federal model risk guidance, now SR 26-2.

Yasir is also an entrepreneur. In 2008, in the middle of the financial crisis, he founded Malik LLC and bought his first rental property while most investors were leaving the Florida market. He has since grown the company to ten units, primarily in Florida, running acquisitions, renovations and a remote operations team alongside a full-time banking career. Owning risk, not only examining it, shapes how he approaches audit and governance.

Yasir holds a B.Sc. (Honours) in Banking and Finance from the London School of Economics, earned on a University of London merit scholarship, and an MBA and a Graduate Advanced Diploma in International Banking from FIU. As an MBA student he was a lab assistant in the Capital Markets Lab, then the State Farm Financial Literacy Lab, and held a competitive FIU Ignite graduate assistantship. He completed bank examination training at the FDIC Corporate University and Columbia Engineering's FinTech program. In September 2025, Dr. Sumit K. Kundu invited him back to FIU to guest-lecture the International MBA class on AI, culture and the political economy.

In 2026, Yasir stepped back from corporate work to focus on his doctorate, and that time is reshaping his research. He studies cognitive bias in audit judgment, and what happens to that judgment when an AI system offers a conclusion that agrees with the auditor's own. He served as Vice President of the Business Doctoral Student Association and is developing Audit the Algorithm, a governance-first AI advisory practice.

Yasir welcomes the chance to connect and exchange ideas with fellow cohort members across industries. His research in progress is at github.com/AuditingAI/Profile, and he can be reached on LinkedIn at linkedin.com/in/yasiramalik.
