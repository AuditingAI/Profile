## 4. Never write

- A DBA completion year or a GPA. Both are unconfirmed and blocked in `CLAUDE.md`.
- "Published", "publication" or "peer-reviewed article" for the AIB-LAC paper. It is accepted for presentation.
- Conference dates for San Juan until the chapter confirms them.
- "Findings", "results show" or "my research proves" about the AI extension. Nothing has been tested.
- That the dissertation is complete or defended.
- A total-years-of-experience number.
- The OCC as his examiner employer. The wording is: bank examiner with the Florida Office of Financial
  Regulation, examining state-chartered banks jointly with the FDIC and the Federal Reserve Bank of Atlanta.
- Phone, email or address. Use `{{PHONE}}` and `{{EMAIL}}`; this repository is public.
- Em-dashes in anything he sends.
