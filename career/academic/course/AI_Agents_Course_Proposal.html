Course proposal · draft for discussion · ISBA, FIU College of Business

AI Agents for Business Applications

A 12-week applied course in which students build, test and govern AI agents that do real business work.

LevelGraduate elective · MSIS Business AI track, Professional MBA AI specialization
Format12 weeks · online or hybrid · one live session weekly · 8 to 10 hours a week
PrerequisiteISM 6444 or equivalent Python; a no-code lab path for non-programmers
Also fitsAn undergraduate version after ISM 4421, or an Executive Education certificate

Why FIU, and why now

Demand is here

The Business Analytics with AI program launched in fall 2025 and reached nearly 1,000 students. They will want to build with AI, not only discuss it.

The next step after strategy

ISM 4421 and ISM 6138 teach AI for business and AI strategy. This course is the hands-on step that follows: agents that retrieve, reason, use tools and act.

Peers are already there

UT Austin McCombs runs a 12-week postgraduate program in AI Agents for Business Applications (USD 3,450). FIU can offer the same skills inside a degree, at a Miami price and scale.

FIU’s edge: governance built in

Every build is tested, logged and reviewed by a human before it counts. Students leave able to deploy an agent and to defend it to an auditor, a regulator or a board, which is what employers in finance, healthcare and government now ask for.

Learning outcomes By the end, students can

  1. Explain generative AI, large language models and agents, and where each fits a business problem
  2. Ground an agent in company documents with retrieval-augmented generation
  3. Build agents that use tools, memory and multi-step reasoning, in code and in no-code platforms
  4. Design a multi-agent workflow and judge when one agent is enough
  5. Test and evaluate an agent for accuracy, reliability and safety, with human review
  6. Govern an agent: privacy, access, decision logs, model risk and a business case leaders can approve

Course arc

Weeks 1 to 4FoundationsGenerative AI, prompting, RAG, first project
Weeks 5 to 8Building agentsTools, memory, MCP, planning and reasoning
Weeks 9 to 10Advanced systemsMulti-agent design, testing and evaluation

Tools

Python, Google Colab, LangChain and LangGraph, the Model Context Protocol, ChromaDB or FAISS, Streamlit, n8n for no-code builds, and current model APIs (Claude, Gemini, OpenAI). Tools are chosen within FIU’s approved list and data rules; no confidential data goes to public platforms.
