## Resume brief

**Function mismatch — read first.** The package on file, `GoldmanSachs_IA_TechRiskCyber_VP`, is
built for Internal Audit technology risk and cyber. This posting is model validation in the Risk
division. Those are different lines of defense with different verbs, different deliverables, and
different readers. Do not submit the audit package unedited.

**Lead with:** JPMorgan Chase Capital Controller — Basel III RWA and CCAR across derivatives, loan,
and securities portfolios, $180M capital optimization identified · Citi RAG review assistant
(~35% cycle-time reduction) built and deployed · AI governance framework referencing NIST AI RMF
and SR 11-7 · DBA qualifying exam passed July 2026, automation bias in professional judgment.

**Then:** Citi VP Audit Manager — thematic audits across 15+ business units, Board Audit Committee
reporting · OCC and state examiner · RRP with Fed and FDIC submissions, one line.

**Reframe:** move the whole document from *audit* voice to *validation* voice. Audit says
"assessed, tested, reported, raised issues"; validation says "independently challenged, tested
model assumptions and limitations, assessed conceptual soundness, documented findings and
conditions of use." Three specific swaps:
- Cyber and technology-risk audit content is the wrong domain here — replace it with the
  quantitative and regulatory-reporting content from the JPMorgan Chase capital work, which is the
  nearest thing on the record to model output under review.
- The Citi AI build should be presented as evidence of technical depth on AI/ML systems, not as an
  audit efficiency win. Name what was built and what it fails at, not just the 35%.
- Position the examiner background as independent challenge experience — it is the honest
  second-line analogue and it is a genuine differentiator against candidates who have only sat
  inside one bank.

**Cut:** cyber, penetration testing, and IT general controls material · Scrum/agile content ·
Sarbanes-Oxley and audit-methodology detail · pre-2010 beyond title and dates.

**Mirror from the posting:** model validation · model risk · AI/ML models · machine learning ·
conceptual soundness · independent challenge · model lifecycle · model documentation · SR 11-7 ·
second line of defense.

**Omit:** DBA completion year and GPA — both unresolved. Any suggestion the dissertation is
finished or that its AI extension has produced findings. Do not overstate hands-on quantitative
modeling — the credible claim is having built and deployed an AI system and understanding its
failure surface, not having developed statistical models.
