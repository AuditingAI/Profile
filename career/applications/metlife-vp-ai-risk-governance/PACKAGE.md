**Reframe for this role:** the `AI_Governance` package is generic, so this is a build. The whole
build turns on translation out of bank vocabulary.

- **Name NIST AI RMF first.** It is regulator-neutral and is what an insurance risk function
  recognizes. SR 11-7 appears once, as the banking analogue, never as the anchor.
- **Regulatory reporting** → producing analytic output that has to be defensible to an external
  examiner. Do not lead with FR Y-14Q / FFIEC 101 / Pillar 3; compress to one line.
- **Basel III / CCAR** → capital and risk models under supervisory review. One line.
- **Audit issue management** → model lifecycle gates and remediation tracking.
- **Board Audit Committee reporting** → governance reporting to executive and board committees.
- **Examiner background** → knowing how an outside supervisor tests a control before they test it.
  This is the strongest transferable asset for a carrier; state it as method, not as jurisdiction.

**Cut:** Basel / CCAR / FR Y-14Q / FFIEC 101 / Pillar 3 line-item detail · RRP to one line · Scrum
and agile material · pre-2010 beyond title and dates.

**Mirror from the posting:** AI risk · responsible AI · model governance · model risk · model
inventory · model lifecycle · NIST AI RMF · second line of defense · risk appetite · policy and
standards · regulatory engagement · cross-functional stakeholders.

**Omit:** DBA completion year and GPA — both unresolved. Any claim that the dissertation or its AI
