## Lead with (top third of page one, in this order)

1. **Citi RAG review assistant — built, deployed, ~35% review cycle-time reduction.** This is the
   single most differentiating line. Almost no governance candidate has shipped one.
2. **AI governance framework proposal — NIST AI RMF + SR 11-7.** Names the two frameworks the JD
   will name.
3. **DBA qualifying exam passed, July 2026 — automation bias in professional judgment.** Research
   credibility on the exact risk the role governs.

## Keep, compressed

- JPMorgan Chase Capital Controller — Basel III RWA, CCAR, $180M capital optimization
- Citi VP Audit Manager — 15+ business units, Board Audit Committee reporting
- OCC / state bank examiner — one line; it is a credibility marker, not a career phase

## Cut or shrink

- Resolution and Recovery Planning detail — strong material, wrong role. One line, not a block.
- Anything Scrum/agile-delivery flavored — dilutes the governance signal here.
- Pre-2010 detail beyond title, employer, dates.

## Language to mirror from the posting

Model risk · model governance · AI/ML lifecycle · control design · independent validation ·
regulatory engagement · automation. Use the posting's nouns, not synonyms — the first pass is
keyword matching, and "AI governance" and "AI/ML governance" do not always match each other.

## Do not include

- DBA completion year (unconfirmed — official record indicates 2028, older resumes say 2027)
- GPA (official 3.81 vs 3.87 on older resumes)
- Any claim the dissertation is complete or that its AI extension has findings

Both discrepancies are unresolved. Omitting is safe; guessing is not, and a bank verifies education.
