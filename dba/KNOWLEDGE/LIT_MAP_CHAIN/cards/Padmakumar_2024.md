# Padmakumar & He (2024)

**Citation.** Padmakumar, V., & He, H. (2024). Does writing with language models reduce content diversity? *The Twelfth International Conference on Learning Representations (ICLR 2024).*
**DOI/URL.** https://proceedings.iclr.cc/paper_files/paper/2024/hash/02dec8877fb7c6aa9a79f81661baca7c-Abstract-Conference.html · arXiv https://arxiv.org/abs/2309.05196
**Source read.** Abstract (arXiv record).
**Already in repo?** No.

**Big idea.** Feedback-tuned models homogenise co-written text; base models do not.
**Design and sample.** Controlled experiment: users wrote argumentative essays with GPT-3, InstructGPT, or no model. Sample details not read.
**Key findings (as stated).** Writing with InstructGPT (not GPT-3) significantly reduced diversity, increasing similarity between different authors' writing; the effect was mainly attributable to InstructGPT contributing less diverse text, while user-contributed text was unaffected.
**Link(s).** L3, partly: convergence across users through a shared model.
**Limitation.** Essays, lay writers, one round.
**Critique angle.** Convergence came from the model's text, not from people changing. That splits L3 into two testable versions: outputs converge vs judgments converge. Only the first is shown here.
