# Doshi & Hauser (2024)

**Citation.** Doshi, A. R., & Hauser, O. P. (2024). Generative AI enhances individual creativity but reduces the collective diversity of novel content. *Science Advances.*
**DOI/URL.** https://doi.org/10.1126/sciadv.adn5290 · https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11244532 · preprint https://arxiv.org/abs/2312.00506
**Source read.** Full-text passages of the arXiv preprint (design, similarity results, methods); published abstract truncated. Figures below are from the preprint.
**Already in repo?** No.

**Big idea.** AI help makes each person's output better and everyone's output more alike.
**Design and sample.** Preregistered two-phase online experiment: 293 writers (Prolific, UK) randomised to human only, 1 GenAI idea, or up to 5 GenAI ideas (GPT-4); 600 evaluators.
**Key findings (as stated, preprint).** AI ideas raised evaluated novelty by 6.7% and usefulness by 6.4%. Writers with AI ideas wrote stories about 5% more similar to the AI ideas, which the authors describe as anchoring on the GenAI idea, and each story was more similar to the average of other stories in its condition.
**Link(s).** L1, partly (similarity to AI idea measured as anchoring). L3, partly: convergence across people via a shared model, one round.
**Limitation.** Creative writing, lay sample, single round.
**Critique angle.** The embedding-similarity measure (to the model's suggestion and to peers) is a ready-made L3 metric for audit memos or risk assessments.
