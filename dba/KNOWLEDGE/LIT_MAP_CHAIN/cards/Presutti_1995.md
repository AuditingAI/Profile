# Presutti (1995)

**Citation.** Presutti, A. H. (1995). Anchor and adjustment heuristic effect on audit judgement. *Managerial Auditing Journal, 10*(9), 13-21.
**DOI/URL.** https://doi.org/10.1108/02686909510100850
**Source read.** Abstract (via search-index extract of the Emerald record). Full text not read.
**Already in repo?** No.

**Big idea.** Tests anchoring in continuing audit engagements, using the previous year's compliance-test results as the potential anchor.
**Design and sample.** Practitioners from public accounting and a government audit service estimated a total population error rate given current-year sample error rates and prior-year results. n not seen.
**Key findings (as stated).** Including the prior year's information appeared to moderate (weaken) the anchoring effect and lowered confidence that the sample error rate represented an acceptable population error rate.
**Link(s).** L1, partly: prior-year anchoring in recurring engagements, the exact setting of the chain, but with a human-generated anchor.
**Limitation.** Older, single journal, abstract-level reading only.
**Critique angle.** Counter-evidence: prior-year information did not simply anchor. The chain needs to explain why system output would behave differently from prior-year working papers.
