# Collaboration intake · AI-assisted mathematics and human verification · 21 Nov 2026

Purpose: the first questions to settle with a mathematics PhD collaborator before applying to Anthropic's
AI for Science program (credits for academic research; welcomes mathematics; reviewed monthly). The
collaborator leads the mathematics and applies from an academic email; Yasir leads the verification and
human-judgment strand. Collaborator details stay out of this public file.

## Round 1 · Fit (send first)
1. What is your research area, and who is your advisor?
2. Is there an open problem, conjecture or stuck lemma in your area that AI assistance could plausibly move?
   A bounded problem is better than a famous one.
3. Would your advisor support you applying as the lead researcher, and should they be named on the application?
4. Have you used Lean, Coq or another proof assistant? Is your area covered by Mathlib?
5. How many hours a week could you give this from January to May 2027?

## Round 2 · Design (after a call)
6. What would count as progress: a proof, a counterexample, a partial result, or a formalized known result?
7. How will each AI-produced step be checked: by you, by Lean, or both? Who signs off?
8. Which models and tools, and roughly how many credits (tokens) would the work need per month?
9. Where would results go: an arXiv preprint, a journal in your field, a conference?
10. Authorship and roles: who writes the mathematics, who writes the verification study?

## The verification strand (Yasir's contribution)
- Log every AI-proposed step: accepted, rejected or revised, by whom, after what check.
- Record when human reading and formal checking disagree, and why.
- Research question: when experts work with an AI that produces proofs, what makes them trust or reject a step,
  and does the AI's confidence anchor their judgment? This extends the anchor, confirm, converge chain from audit
  review to mathematics.
- Ethics: if the log becomes data about people, it needs IRB review before it is used in research.

## Rules
- Never claim a result before it is checked. Say "claimed" until verified.
- Disclose AI use as FIU policy and the collaborator's institution require.
