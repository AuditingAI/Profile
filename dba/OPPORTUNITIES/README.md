## The triage rule — three questions, sixty seconds

When something lands on the listserv:

1. **Does the deadline clash with a course deadline?** Check `../coursework/` first. The 18 September
   collision was found this way and would otherwise have been found on the 18th.
2. **Does it need something that does not exist yet** — a letter from Rey, a CV, a committee
   signature? If so the real deadline is earlier than the stated one, by however long that takes.
3. **Would a chair reading this in three years care?** If not, skip it without guilt. A cluttered
   CV of small service is worse than a short one with two real things.

If it survives all three, it gets a file in this directory and a row in the table above.

## What must never be claimed on any application

This is a public repository and applications go to people who can check.

- **No DBA completion year and no GPA** until the program office confirms them. Both are unresolved.
- **No claim of publications that do not exist.** The manuscript is drafted, not submitted.
- **No claim that the AI extension has findings.** It has a design and an argument.
- **No teaching evaluations, no memberships** that are not confirmed.
