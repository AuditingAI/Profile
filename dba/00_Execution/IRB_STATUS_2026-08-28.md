## 2 · The qualitative arm — the live one

This is the item that matters this term, because GEB 7911's proposal is built on it.

| | Approved under IRB-25-0462 | What the qualitative arm actually does |
|---|---|---|
| Method | Anonymous online survey | Semi-structured interview, **audio-recorded** |
| Data | Likert responses, no identifiers | Recordings and transcripts — **identifiable until de-identified** |
| Consent | Online, implied by submission | Verbal recorded consent, plus separate recording consent |
| Risk | Minimal | Minimal, but professional-reputation exposure is real |

**The risk that has to be addressed rather than asserted away:** participants describe their own
judgment practices, and some will describe practices their firm would not endorse. Confidentiality is
not a formality in this design.

**The three questions to put to the FIU IRB office — they are questions, not a submission**, and
asking them costs nothing:

- [ ] Does FIU treat this as a **modification** to IRB-25-0462 or a **new protocol**? This determines
      the entire timeline and should be settled before a word of the application is drafted.
- [ ] Must the **outsider reviewer** be named on the protocol?
- [ ] May transcription use an **automated service**, and if so which ones are approved? FIU UGS
      policy §9 forbids uploading identifiable data to a public AI platform, so this is not a free
      choice.

Controls already specified in `../QUALITATIVE/SAMPLING_AND_RECRUITMENT.md` §5: pseudonyms at
transcription, linking key held separately and destroyed at close, no firm or client names in
transcripts, encrypted recordings deleted after member check, withdrawal of any passage without
reason.

**Compensation must be decided before submission, not after.** Adding it later is its own amendment.
