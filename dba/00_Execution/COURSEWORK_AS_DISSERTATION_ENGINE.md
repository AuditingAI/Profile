# The two courses and Dr. Rey's five directives — what is actually covered

**Compiled 28 August 2026.** Scholar lane. Fall 2026 runs two courses that touch the dissertation:
**GEB 7365 International Business** (Newburry) and **GEB 7911 Qualitative Research Methods**
(Gonzalez). This file maps what they genuinely advance against
[`Rey_Final_Feedback_Dissertation_Plan.md`](Rey_Final_Feedback_Dissertation_Plan.md) — and, more
usefully, what they do not.

**Read the second table before the first.** The optimistic reading of this term is that coursework
quietly discharges the dissertation debt. It does not, and believing it would cost a semester.

---

## The constraint being worked around

The qualifying study fielded and did not reach its sample. Against a target of n = 100: **23 raw
responses, four usable** *(the exclusion log records "four to five" depending on one borderline case;
the analysis proceeded at n = 4)*. Applying the eligibility criteria to a research panel of 334,976
members returned approximately **twenty** eligible people — a prevalence near six per hundred
thousand.

Dr. Rey passed the study and singled out the honest treatment of that failure as its strongest
feature: *"Rather than simply acknowledging the limitation, you developed well-supported
recommendations for future research."* His fifth directive follows directly — **build a comprehensive
recruitment strategy before beginning the dissertation.**

The relevant fact about this term is that **GEB 7365 requires a data-collection plan and explicitly
requires no collected data.** That is the first time the recruitment constraint can be worked on
without being blocked by it.

## What the two courses genuinely advance

| Rey's directive | Course | What it actually does | Honest strength |
|---|---|---|---|
| **5 · Recruitment strategy before the dissertation** | GEB 7365 | The project requires a written data-collection plan with no fielding. The chosen topic *is* the feasibility problem — prevalence, reachable n, and when a comparative design stops being viable | **Strong and direct.** This is the one place coursework lands squarely on a directive |
| **1 · Expand Chapter 2 · 2 · Reorganize by construct · 4 · Deepen each hypothesis** | GEB 7365 | The final paper needs 2–3 hypotheses justified from literature, validated scales, method named. Same craft — derive a hypothesis from a synthesis rather than assert it | **Transferable practice only.** Different topic, different literature. It rehearses the skill; it does not produce the chapter |
| **— (not a Rey directive)** | GEB 7911 | The qualitative proposal is 50% of that grade and is the L2 phenomenology arm — protocol, sampling, coding plan, trustworthiness criteria, audit trail. Gonzalez recommended phenomenology and the design follows it | **Strong.** It builds a real arm of the dissertation, but it answers a question Rey's directives do not cover |
| **3 · Terminology sequencing** | Neither | — | Nothing. It is a revision pass on existing text |

**The one genuinely elegant thing about this term:** the two courses can run different arms of the
same research honestly. Gonzalez gets the qualitative proposal (L2). Newburry gets the quantitative
design and the feasibility argument. Neither is padded, neither is double-submitted, and both are
real coursework.

## What the two courses do NOT cover — read this one twice

| Claim it would be easy to drift into | Why it is false |
|---|---|
| *"Coursework is handling the data problem"* | **Neither course collects a single response.** The achieved n is still four. The prevalence constraint is unchanged. Writing well about why a population is hard to reach does not make it reachable |
| *"Chapters 2 and 3 are being rebuilt through the courses"* | They are not. Directives 1, 2 and 4 require rebuilding **his** literature review around **his** eleven constructs. A GEB 7365 paper on a different topic rehearses the method and produces none of the text. **Chapters 4–6 have been owed to Dr. Rey since 28 July** |
| *"The GEB 7911 proposal is the dissertation's qualitative arm, approved"* | It is a course deliverable. **A course grade is not committee approval**, and the arm it describes has no IRB clearance — see [`IRB_STATUS_2026-08-28.md`](IRB_STATUS_2026-08-28.md) |
| *"The IRB is being handled"* | Three of the four IRB items have never been submitted and one has never been confirmed filed |
| *"The GEB 7365 topic solves the feasibility problem"* | It **studies** the problem. Turning a constraint into a research object is a genuine contribution and also a way of living with something unsolved. Both things are true and the second one should not be forgotten |
| *"AI use in this work is disclosed and cleared"* | Disclosure is being done. **FIU UGS §3.2 also requires the committee to review and approve the AI use, and that has never been requested of Dr. Rey** |
