## 2 · What this does for the programme, honestly ranked

**The most important thing is one word.** She uses **"epistemic drift"** in a title. The Scholar
alert audit of 26 August scanned 1,637 papers and found that term **zero** times. The only prior
external use of the L3 idea in this repository is the EFT paper on LLMs auditing each other, which
is still unread. Kinne is the second, and she uses the exact phrase. That does not make L3
established. It means the term has a life outside this repository, which matters for two reasons:
it is citable as a framing once her work is read, and it means a search for the term will now
return something.

**Second, she supplies what L3 lacks: a mechanism.** L3 as written says successive systems
reprocess earlier machine-influenced work and converge on each other rather than on evidence. It
does not say *why* they converge. "Structural inevitability... RLHF, MDL compression" is a why. If
her argument holds up on reading, L3 stops being a description of a pattern and becomes a
prediction from a cause, which is the difference between a hypothesis and an observation.

**Third, her measurement critique is his measurement critique, one level up.** The status deck's
slide 7 says construct 11 measures the wrong thing: self-report cannot capture a bias that
prevents self-observation. Her post says the field's outcome measure, output variance, cannot
capture drift that lengthens with reasoning chains. Same shape of error. His memo can cite hers as
the field-level version once read.

**Fourth, "cognitive integrity" is a candidate name for the L3 outcome construct.** He has no
outcome construct for L3. "Preservation of human reasoning quality over time" is one, stated by
someone else, which is better than inventing it.

## 3 · Where to be careful

- **She is not peer-reviewed.** These are essays on a personal site. They are leads for framing and
  for her own citations, not sources to put in a Chapter 2 table. The repository's rule stands: a
  lead is not a citation. Her posts go in the reading list, not the reference list, until read
  and until her sources are traced.
- **Her "epistemic drift" may not be his.** Hers reads as drift in a model's reasoning under
  optimisation pressure. His is drift in the *evidentiary basis of a human judgment* across
  engagements. Related, possibly the same, possibly not. Read before equating.
- **Do not cite the blog for the mechanism.** If "RLHF + MDL compression → drift" is a real
  argument, it has a real literature behind it, and that is what gets cited. Her post is the map
  to that literature, not the destination.
