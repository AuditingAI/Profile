## 🔴 Do not send this yet. Two minutes on the dashboard comes first

**An email cannot release the money if the study is still running.** Prolific charges only for
approved submissions; the rest of the committed budget returns to your workspace balance **when the
study stops**. While it runs, the money stays escrowed and no support request can reach it.
**Stopping the study is the action. The email is the follow-up.**
