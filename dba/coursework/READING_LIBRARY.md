# Reading library — how the papers are obtained, stored, and tracked

The single entry point for coursework reading. **Read this file before opening any other coursework
file.** It is deliberately short so that starting a session is cheap.

---

## 1. You do not need to buy a single article

Every assigned article in both courses is available **free** through FIU, and the GEB 7365 syllabus
says so in as many words:

> *"The FIU Online Library is available to you to access and download your assigned readings…
> To access the library, click on 'off-campus access' in the upper right-hand corner of the page.
> You can log in to the FIU Library resources using your FIU email address and password."*

**Three routes, in order of speed:**

1. **Canvas first.** GEB 7365: *"Articles and other readings required for the course will be provided
   via Canvas or in the class."* Most PDFs are already posted. Check Modules before searching.
2. **FIU Online Library → off-campus access → sign in with the FIU account.** FIU subscribes to the
   journals every one of these papers is in — *JIBS*, *SMJ*, *Organization Science*, *JWB*, *MIR*,
   *JIM*, *GSJ*, *HBR*, *IBR*. Search the article title; download the PDF.
3. **Google Scholar with FIU library links enabled.** In Scholar → Settings → Library links → add
   *Florida International University*. Every result then shows a "Find it @ FIU" link that goes
   straight through the paywall. Ten-minute setup, saves hours across a term.

**Do not pay a publisher $40 for an article FIU already licenses.** If a paper looks paywalled, the
sign-in didn't carry — go back through off-campus access.

## 2. The one thing to actually buy

**Creswell, J. W., & Poth, C. N. (2024). *Qualitative Inquiry and Research Design: Choosing Among
Five Approaches*, 5th ed. Sage.** — required for GEB 7911.

Order of preference:
1. **FIU Library** — check for an e-book licence first. Sage titles are frequently held. Free if so.
2. **Sage's own site or a rental service** — a term rental typically runs a fraction of purchase.
3. **Buy used.** The 4th edition is substantially the same on the five approaches and much cheaper;
   fine as a stopgap while the 5th arrives, but page and chapter numbers will not match citations.

*Verify the ISBN on the seller's page against author, title and edition before ordering — do not
order from an ISBN quoted in any AI-generated document, including this one. No ISBN is stated here
for that reason.*

GEB 7365 has **no textbook**. Articles only.

## 4. The note card — one format, used everywhere

Three lines is the whole discipline. Written once, it serves class participation, the memo, the
topic-area presentation, and the literature review.

```markdown
# Author (Year) — Short Title
**Journal**, vol(issue): pages · Course/session · Read: YYYY-MM-DD

**Claim.**          What the paper argues, in one sentence.
**Contribution.**   What it added that was not there before.
**Leaves open.**    The gap it names or the one it walks past.

**Use.**            Where this goes — which assignment, which chapter, which argument.
**Quote.**          One sentence worth citing verbatim, with page number.
```

Why this shape: Newburry's topic-area assignment requires *summary of findings*, *critical
assessment*, and *relation to other topics* — that is Claim / Leaves open / Use. Gonzalez's memos
require insight rather than summary — that is Leaves open. The format is not admin; it is the
assignments' own structure.

## 6. Standing rules

- **Never** commit a licensed PDF to this public repository.
- **Never** cite a paper from a note card alone in graded work — reopen the PDF and check the page.
- A paper with no note card **has not been read**, regardless of how it felt at the time.
- Every note card records the date read, so staleness is visible.
