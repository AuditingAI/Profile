## The thread through all five

The multinational is **less unified than the label suggests**. Sales are regional, governance is
local, subsidiaries push back, and control is harder than the org chart implies. Every one of these
papers is about a **gap between the label and the reality**.

That is also the shape of the dissertation argument, which is worth saying out loud in the
presentation: an AI system that passes its own validation while the judgment around it degrades is
the same gap, in a different setting.
