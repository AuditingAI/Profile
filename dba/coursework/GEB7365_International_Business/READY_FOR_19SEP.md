## What he will ask on Saturday, and what you say

**"Isn't this a methods paper in an international business frame?"**
You asked him this yourself on 1 September and he reserved judgement. The answer is that the
dependent variable is cross-national: which national frames can be reached, and why coverage differs
between them. **H2 makes it an IB paper specifically**, because it applies the regionalization thesis
to a new class of firm. If he still says methods, ask him where in the IB methodology literature to
take it. You had that question in the Initial Project Idea and it was never answered.

**"How would you actually measure a panel's home region?"**
Country of incorporation and of principal operations, mapped onto the triad classification Rugman
and Verbeke use, which is why that classification is in the paper rather than a generic notion of
region. Providers are selected to vary on it deliberately. **H2 is unidentifiable if every provider
is American**, and saying that before he does shows the design was thought through.

**"Your prevalence comes from one study in one country. Why hold it constant?"**
Because no cross-national prevalence data for these populations exists, and obtaining it is the
contribution rather than an assumption. Held constant, the model isolates the response-rate effect.
**The cross-national figures illustrate a structure. They do not estimate a quantity.** That is in
the limitations and you should say it before he asks.

**"What if the big panels have all converged on global recruitment?"**
Then H2 fails, and that is worth documenting, because it would bound the regionalization thesis at
the point where infrastructure stops behaving like a firm. A hypothesis that is interesting when it
fails is a better hypothesis. This answer is already in the discussion section.

**"The Korea number is a confound, not a finding."**
Agreed, and that is the point. One observation is not evidence. It is the observation that makes H4
worth testing. If mode dominates country, part of what the comparative literature reads as national
context is instrument selection. **If it does not, the finding still disciplines a number that sits
in a widely cited source without qualification.**

**"Why thirty usable responses per country?"**
It is a convention, not a derivation, and it is stated as a target in the sensitivity analysis
rather than a requirement. The result does not depend on it. The binding-frame structure holds at
any threshold, because it is a property of the conjunction and not of the number.

**"What is the dependent variable?"**
Reachable usable sample: frame size × prevalence × response rate × screen survival. Observable
before fielding, on the panel's own interface, at no cost. **That is the whole argument for calling
it a parameter rather than a limitation.**
