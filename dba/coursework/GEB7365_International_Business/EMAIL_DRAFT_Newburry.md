## Why it is shaped this way

**It leads with the fact that changes everything — individual — before anything else.** He already
knows this from class, but confirming it in writing means there is a paper trail independent of a
transcript.

**It proposes a specific time rather than asking for availability.** "Does Tuesday 3pm work" is a
yes/no he can answer in five seconds. "When are you free" is a scheduling round-trip.

**The topic paragraph is unchanged from the version written for Group B**, because the argument
never depended on having a partner — it is Yasir's own dissertation problem read as an IB question.
Going solo removes a compromise that was never actually needed.

**It closes on something true and specific** — the class transcript's own note that the presentation
was "well-coordinated for a first presentation" — rather than a generic pleasantry.
