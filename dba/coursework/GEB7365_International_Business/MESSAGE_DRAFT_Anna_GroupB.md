## Why it is shaped this way

**It leads with what he brings, then hands her the floor.** Saying "I would rather hear yours first"
and meaning it is the single best thing you can do in a first message to a new collaborator.

**It names the constraint that makes the project doable** — 2–3 hypotheses, a data collection plan,
no actual data — so she knows you have read the brief.

**It mentions the conference without pushing it.** Interactive/poster track, deadline moved a month
earlier for visa processing. Worth planting; not worth arguing about on day one.

**Keep the offer genuine.** If her topic is better, take it. A group project you are enthusiastic
about beats one you own.
