## The four general instructions

### 1 · "Level of analysis must be clearly stated"

Given to Group B, then repeated as general guidance.

| Before | After |
|---|---|
| "Unit of analysis" in small type, one row of one table on slide 10 | A gold **LEVEL OF ANALYSIS** strip on the model slide, slide 4, directly under the opening paragraph. Restated as the first row of the data-sources table on slide 9 |

The statement itself: **one occupation, on one provider, in one national frame. Cells nested in
national frames, frames nested in regions, providers cross-classified.** Three levels, said in words,
twice.

### 2 · "Integrate citations into the slide text"

Given to Group B. The reason given: at an academic conference the audience cannot read a footnote
list, and they are actively interested in sources.

| Before | After |
|---|---|
| Sources in a right-hand column on the hypotheses slide, set apart from the sentence, which reads as a footnote list | The citation is inside the sentence: *"Following Rugman and Verbeke (2004) on home-region orientation, specialist prevalence on a panel is higher inside the panel provider's home region"* |

The right-hand column is gone. The space it freed went to the sentences.

### 3 · "Spell out every acronym the first time it is used"

Raised twice on 19 September.

## Three things said to other groups that land on this design

### "Relabel to avoid ambiguity" · said to Group A

They were told to say *positive moderation* rather than *weakening*, because a reader cannot tell
what the weaker word means. H1 had the same problem: it said a country **can only weakly decrease**
joint feasibility, where *weakly* could be read as *slightly*.

> **Now:** "Adding a country leaves it unchanged or lowers it, and can never raise it."
> The sign column label changed from MONOTONE to **NEVER RISES**.

### "H1 and H2 are not the contribution, they are replication steps" · said to Group A

The deck never said which hypothesis carries the contribution. It does now, under the table:

> **THE CONTRIBUTION IS H2.** H1 is the structural result the design rests on. H2 carries the
> international business contribution, applying the regionalization thesis to research infrastructure.

### "The country is the setting, not a variable" · said to Group C

Group C was told a country-level variable has no variation when only one country is studied. This
design does have variation, but the point is now made before it is asked:

> **REGION IS A VARIABLE, NOT A SETTING.** Providers chosen to vary on home region. With one region
> there is no variation and H2 is unidentifiable.

## Not changed, and why

- **The model stays.** Newburry's own instruction on 10 September was that the model can sit before
  or after the hypotheses but must not be left out.
- **No firm size or profitability controls.** He named those as almost always necessary in
  **firm-level** studies. The unit here is a cell, not a firm, and frame size already carries the
  scale term. Worth raising with him rather than adding silently.
