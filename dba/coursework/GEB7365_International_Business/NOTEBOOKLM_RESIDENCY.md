## The one line to carry out of the session

**Fainshmidt may have changed my H2.** I have been operationalising "region" on Rugman and Verbeke's
triad. If what actually determines whether a panel can reach a specialist professional population is
the configuration of human capital institutions, social capital and the character of the state, then
**two countries inside one triad region can differ more than two countries across regions.**

Testing H2 against **seven institutional system types** rather than three geographic regions is a
sharper design, and it is defensible rather than convenient, because the taxonomy already exists and
was built for exactly the economies a commercial panel is least likely to cover.

**Ask Newburry about this.** Fainshmidt is at FIU and Deephouse and Newburry wrote one of the five.
