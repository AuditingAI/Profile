## If a proxy link fails

**Use OneSearch instead of fighting the link.** It is more reliable than any constructed URL:

1. `library.fiu.edu` → **OneSearch**
2. Paste the **article title in quotes**
3. Sign in with your FIU credentials when prompted
4. **Full Text Online** → PDF

**Or use the EZProxy URL Generator** — FIU's own tool that builds the prefixed link for you:
https://library.fiu.edu/faculty/ezproxy

**Or ask a librarian.** FIU's business librarian will send a PDF within a day, and if FIU genuinely
does not hold something, **interlibrary loan is free and usually turns around in 24–48 hours.**
