## Feedback given to other groups that lands on your design

### Group A · Daniela, Nicole, Ariadne, Guido — digital provider status moderating CAGE distance

- **"Relabel moderating hypotheses H3 and H4 as positive moderation rather than weakening, to avoid
  ambiguity."** Your H1 currently says a country can only *weakly decrease* joint feasibility. That
  is the same ambiguity: a reader cannot tell whether *weakly* means *slightly* or means *in the weak
  sense of the inequality*. It means the second. Say so in words a reader cannot misread.
- **"H1 and H2 are not the contribution. They are replication steps needed to test the moderator."**
  Applied to yours: H1 is the structural result and H2 is the international business contribution.
  The deck does not currently say which one carries the contribution.
- Suggested firm headquarters location rather than country centroid for geographic distance.

### Group C · Nathan, Dave, Anna — greenfield versus acquisition in Mexico

## Final paper guidance, for the 9 October submission

Content mirrors the presentation, formatted as an academic paper.

| Section | Newburry's instruction |
|---|---|
| Introduction | 2 to 3 pages. Topic, why it matters, what you add to the conversation |
| Literature review | The main constructs that underpin the hypotheses. Cite the key papers, do not summarise everything you read |
| Hypotheses | About one page each. Explain the theoretical logic: **why** does A affect B |
| Methodology | Context first, meaning geographic scope, level of analysis, sample size. Then dependent variable, then independent variables, then controls, in that order. Name the statistical method |
| Results | Not required for this class. Include preliminary evidence where you have it |
| Discussion | Expected findings and why they would matter |
| References | Only what is actually cited. Do not add things you read but did not cite |

**Controls:** firm size and firm profitability are almost always necessary in firm-level studies.

## AIB LAC submission mechanics

- **Interactive track, work in progress.** Not Competitive, which requires fully analysed data.
- One author submits, all co-authors listed.
- **Paper must be blinded. No identifying information, including the institution name.**
- Track chairs assign two reviewers.
- Roughly 100 dollars early bird before 30 January, 40 to 50 dollars membership, plus airfare and
  hotel. A grant of up to 500 dollars is available.
- At least one author must register for an accepted paper to appear on the programme.

## Insights.AIB.World, the applied journal

- About 2,500 words, 3,500 maximum. 8 to 15 references. Figures and tables do not count.
- Abstract 100 words, introduction about 250, foundation and literature about 1,000,
  **recommendations is the largest section**, short conclusion.
- State upfront who you are writing for, managers or policymakers or educators, and what actionable
  insight they get.
- Acceptance around 33 percent. Review cycle about 45 days per round. Six issues a year.
- **Newburry cannot handle submissions from current FIU students** because of the conflict of
  interest. Another editor manages those.
