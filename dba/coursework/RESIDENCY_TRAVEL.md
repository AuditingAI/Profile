*Scholar lane. Covers travel to the four Fall 2026 FIU MMC residencies. Confirmation numbers,
account numbers, and card digits are deliberately **not** in this file — this repository is public.
Those live in the calendar events and in Notion.*

**Rule: never fly in the morning of Day 1.** A single delay costs a full graded residency day in a
course where participation is 25% of the grade.

## Airport choice

**MIA over FLL.** FLL adds roughly 45–60 minutes each way to FIU MMC. On a 9:30am start with an
8:00am Miami rush hour, that gap is the difference between arriving early and arriving late. FLL is
worth taking only when the fare gap is large enough to cover a rental-car upgrade and the extra
drive time.

## Card strategy — the Sapphire card

**What is actually verified.** From his own inbox: he holds a **Chase Sapphire card** (a Nov 2025
Chase notice about "your Sapphire card's new earning rate" and a Lyft benefit update), he has used
**Pay Yourself Back**, and he is a **Chase Private Client**. Several other Chase cards are on the
same profile; the account digits stay in the calendar and Notion, not here.

**What is NOT verified and must not be asserted:** which Sapphire product it is. Preferred, Reserve,
and Reserve for Business have materially different annual fees, portal multipliers, statement
credits, and insurance limits. Confirm the product name off the physical card or the Chase app
before relying on any specific figure below.

### 1. Book through Chase Travel when the fare is close

Every Sapphire product pays its top rate through the Chase Travel portal and a lower rate for the
same flight bought direct or through an OTA (Expedia, Booking, Priceline). An OTA booking also
forfeits the redemption bonus. **Book through Chase Travel whenever the fare is within about 10% of
the direct fare.** Check the current multipliers in the Chase app — do not assume a number.

### 2. Put the entire fare on the Sapphire card — no splitting

This is the rule that actually costs money when broken. Trip cancellation/interruption, trip delay,
and baggage delay coverage attach **only** to the portion of the fare charged to the card. Paying on
a different card, or splitting the fare, prorates or voids the claim entirely.

*This has already gone wrong once.* The August residency ticket was bought on a non-Sapphire Visa,
so it carries no Chase travel protection at all. It is also a Basic Economy fare, which has no
change or cancellation rights of its own — the least-protected combination available.

### 3. The protections are the real reason to use the card on these trips

Chase's published Sapphire coverage, subject to confirming the specific product:

| Benefit | Trigger | Limit |
|---|---|---|
| Trip delay reimbursement | Common-carrier delay **over 6 hours** or requiring an overnight | up to **$500** per traveler — meals, lodging |
| Trip cancellation / interruption | Sickness, severe weather, other covered reasons | up to **$10,000** per traveler, $20,000 per trip |
| Baggage delay | Delay **over 6 hours** | $100/day, up to 5 days |

For August-through-November travel into South Florida — hurricane season, daily afternoon
convective delays at MIA and FLL — the 6-hour trip-delay trigger is not hypothetical. Keep every
receipt on a delayed leg.

### 4. Credits to check for and use this fall

Confirm which of these the specific product actually carries, then use them:

- **Annual travel credit** — applies automatically to travel purchases. Four residency trips absorb
  it without any planning.
- **Hotel credit through The Edit by Chase Travel** — paid in halves, two-night minimum. A residency
  *is* a two-night stay by construction. Most likely the single most underused credit on the card.
- **Global Entry / TSA PreCheck credit**, every four years. If unused, apply before the November
  residency; PreCheck alone changes the arithmetic on a tight evening departure out of LGA or EWR.

### 5. Where points come from and where they should go

Ultimate Rewards transfer **1:1** to airline and hotel partners. Two that matter here:

- **Southwest Rapid Rewards** — there is an existing Southwest balance sitting idle. Southwest award
  tickets refund to points, which makes them the right instrument for a residency date that could
  still shift. Use points where the schedule is uncertain and cash where it is fixed.
- **World of Hyatt** — usually the strongest cent-per-point redemption among the transfer partners;
  the right home for points if a Miami stay can be covered on award nights.

Do not transfer points speculatively. Transfers are one-way and irreversible.

## Standing checklist for each residency booking

- [ ] Outbound is the **evening before** Day 1, never the morning of
- [ ] Return is after 7:30pm on Day 2, or the following morning
- [ ] MIA unless the FLL saving is large
- [ ] Priced through Chase Travel and compared against direct
- [ ] Full fare on the Sapphire card — never split, never a different card
- [ ] Hotel checked against The Edit inventory first (two-night minimum is satisfied)
- [ ] Calendar event replaced with the real flight times and confirmation
- [ ] Receipts retained for the trip in case a delay claim is needed
