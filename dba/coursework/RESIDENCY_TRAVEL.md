# Residency travel — booking rules and card strategy

*Scholar lane. Covers travel to the four Fall 2026 FIU MMC residencies. Confirmation numbers,
account numbers, and card digits are deliberately **not** in this file — this repository is public.
Those live in the calendar events and in Notion.*

## The residency dates that drive everything

| Residency | Dates | Travel window |
|---|---|---|
| Week 1 | **Fri 21 – Sat 22 Aug** | Out Thu 8/20 PM · back Sat 8/22 late or Sun 8/23 |
| Week 5 | Fri 18 – Sat 19 Sep | Out Thu 9/17 PM · back Sun 9/20 |
| October | Fri 16 – Sat 17 Oct | Out Thu 10/15 PM · back Sun 10/18 |
| November | Fri 13 – Sat 14 Nov | Out Thu 11/12 PM · back Sun 11/15 |

Residency days run **9:30am–5:30pm**, in person, attendance required. Recordings do not substitute.

**Rule: never fly in the morning of Day 1.** A single delay costs a full graded residency day in a
course where participation is 25% of the grade.

---

## Airport choice

**MIA over FLL.** FLL adds roughly 45–60 minutes each way to FIU MMC. On a 9:30am start with an
8:00am Miami rush hour, that gap is the difference between arriving early and arriving late. FLL is
worth taking only when the fare gap is large enough to cover a rental-car upgrade and the extra
drive time.

## Card strategy — Chase Sapphire Reserve for Business

The relevant card is the **Sapphire Reserve for Business** (opened Sept 2025, $795 annual fee).
Its economics only work if the travel actually runs through it. Three rules:

### 1. Book through Chase Travel when the fare is close

| Booking channel | Points per dollar |
|---|---|
| Chase Travel | **8x** |
| Direct with the airline or hotel | 4x |
| An OTA (Expedia, Booking, Priceline) | 1x–4x, and no Points Boost on redemption |

At a conservative 1.5¢/point, 8x is ~12% back and 4x is ~6%. **Book through Chase Travel whenever
the fare is within about 10% of the direct fare.** Booking an OTA gives up the best rate *and* the
redemption bonus for no benefit.

### 2. Put the entire fare on the card — no splitting

Trip cancellation/interruption, trip delay, and baggage delay coverage attach only to the portion of
the fare charged to the card. Paying part in points or on another card prorates or voids the claim.

### 3. The protections are the real reason to use it on this trip

| Benefit | Trigger | Limit |
|---|---|---|
| Trip delay reimbursement | Common-carrier delay **over 6 hours** or requiring an overnight | **$500** per traveler — meals, lodging |
| Trip cancellation / interruption | Sickness, severe weather, other covered reasons | **$10,000** per traveler, $20,000 per trip |
| Baggage delay | Delay **over 6 hours** | $100/day up to 5 days |

For an August–November travel schedule into South Florida — hurricane season, daily afternoon
convective delays at MIA and FLL — the 6-hour trip-delay trigger is not hypothetical. Keep every
receipt on a delayed leg.

### 4. Credits to actually use this fall

- **$300 annual travel credit** — applies automatically to travel purchases. Four residency trips
  will absorb it without any planning.
- **Up to $500/yr hotel credit through The Edit by Chase Travel** ($250 per half, two-night minimum).
  A residency is a two-night stay by construction. Check whether an Edit property near MMC or
  Brickell prices out competitively before booking anything else — this is the single most
  underused credit on the card.
- **$120 Global Entry / TSA PreCheck credit**, every four years. If it has not been used, apply
  before the November residency; PreCheck alone changes the arithmetic on a tight Thursday evening
  departure.

### 5. Where points come from and where they should go

Ultimate Rewards transfer **1:1** to airline and hotel partners. Two that matter here:

- **Southwest Rapid Rewards** — there is an existing Southwest balance sitting idle. Southwest award
  tickets are cash-refundable to points, which makes them the right instrument for a residency date
  that could still shift. Use points where the schedule is uncertain and cash where it is fixed.
- **World of Hyatt** — usually the strongest cent-per-point redemption of the transfer partners; the
  right home for points if a Miami stay can be covered on award nights.

Do not transfer points speculatively. Transfers are one-way and irreversible.

## Standing checklist for each residency booking

- [ ] Outbound is the **evening before** Day 1, never the morning of
- [ ] Return is after 7:30pm on Day 2, or the following morning
- [ ] MIA unless the FLL saving is large
- [ ] Priced through Chase Travel and compared against direct
- [ ] Full fare on the Sapphire Reserve for Business
- [ ] Hotel checked against The Edit inventory first (two-night minimum is satisfied)
- [ ] Calendar event replaced with the real flight times and confirmation
- [ ] Receipts retained for the trip in case a delay claim is needed
