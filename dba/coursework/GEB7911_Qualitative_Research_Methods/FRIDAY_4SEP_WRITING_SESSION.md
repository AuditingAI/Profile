## BLOCK 1 · 25 min — the hook and the problem

**Produce: the first half-page. The part only you can write.**

Do not plan it. Start typing at the first sentence starter below and keep going.

> **Sentence starter, use it literally:**
> *"An auditor reviewing a multi-year engagement has always had a starting point: last year's
> conclusion."*

Then keep going, in your own words, covering:

- What changed — the starting point is now generated by a system
- Three things about it: it is **automatic**, it is **continuous**, it arrives **before** the reviewer
  has formed a view
- And the fourth thing, which is the whole study: **when the reviewer states a position, the system
  tends to agree with it**

**Then the problem statement**, one paragraph. The sentence to land:

> *Professional standards assume a competent human reviewer sits above the system. Nobody has
> measured whether that reviewer's judgment survives contact with it.*

**Then the deficiencies**, three bullets — you already have them:
1. The AI-and-audit empirical literature is thin, and **you have not read it in full** — say what is
   missing, not what you have read
2. Existing work treats AI reliance as a **magnitude** question; this is a **process** question, and
   a survey cannot answer it
3. Your own study measured the outcome by **self-report** — the one thing the phenomenon prevents a
   respondent from noticing
