## For the final proposal paper

Carry all of the above into the methods section, in these words or close to them:
- *Sampling:* criterion plus referral sampling with one screen; the screening question was tested
  first; participants recall the experience without being asked to characterise it.
- *Sample size:* about 10 to 15, judged by the depth and richness of the accounts, with the decision
  and its reasons recorded.
- *Member checking:* scope stated explicitly, informing individual descriptions only.
- *External review:* purposeful selection with memos, including a disconfirming transcript.
- *Hazard log:* entries linked to decisions.
- *AI disclosure:* in the paper as well as on the poster (FIU Graduate School policy; course 25%
  limit and labelling rule).
