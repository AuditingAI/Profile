## Part 3 · Research question  ·  a few lines

**Lead with the central question above — it is already compliant.** Her advice: **start with one
strong RQ.** Subquestions evolve later.

If you include subquestions, **pick two or three, not seven.** The strongest for this memo:

- What do auditors describe as happening when an AI tool confirms a judgment they had already reached?
- How do auditors distinguish, in their own accounts, between **confirmation** and **corroboration**?
- What distinctions do auditors draw between an AI conclusion and a prior-year workpaper as a
  starting point?

*The third is the hinge to your quantitative model. Keep it.*
