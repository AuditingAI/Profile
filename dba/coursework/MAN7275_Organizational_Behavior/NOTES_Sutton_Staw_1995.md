# Note card · Sutton & Staw (1995), What theory is not · MAN 7275 Week 1

Sutton, R. I., & Staw, B. M. (1995). What theory is not. *Administrative Science Quarterly, 40*(3),
371–384. ASQ Forum essay. Read in full by Claude 14 Oct 2026 for Yasir's prep; Yasir to read himself.

**Big idea.** There is little agreement on what *strong* theory is, but wide agreement on what theory is
**not**. Five things routinely stand in for theory: references, data, lists of variables, diagrams and
hypotheses. Each is useful; none explains **why**.

| Not theory | Why not | p. |
|---|---|---|
| References | Naming a theory is not explaining its causal logic | 372 |
| Data | Data show *which* patterns occurred; theory explains *why* (Kaplan, 1964) | 373–374 |
| Lists of variables or constructs | Definitions do not say why constructs arise or connect | 374–375 |
| Diagrams | Boxes and arrows show *that*, rarely *why*; best when they show causal order, mediators, time | 375–376 |
| Hypotheses | Say *what* is expected, not *why*; bridges between theory and data | 376–377 |

**What theory is** (p. 378): "the answer to queries of why"; a story about why acts, events, structures
and thoughts occur, with causal order and timing. Strong theory goes down into micro-processes,
sideways into neighbouring concepts, up to broader phenomena; it can be counter-intuitive. Weick:
good theory "explains, predicts, and delights."

**Proposal for journals** (abstract, p. 371): accept papers that test **part** of a theory and use
**illustrative** rather than definitive data, if the field wants stronger theory.

**Critique angles.** Written from editors' experience, not evidence; mostly about variance theory
(process and interpretive theory get less attention); says what theory is not more clearly than what
it is (they admit their own description reads like "a wish list").

**For his own work.** Test the three-link chain against it: is each link explained (*why* would an AI
output anchor, *why* would sycophancy and automation bias reinforce), or only listed and diagrammed?
The MAN 7275 proposal's hypotheses each need a *why* paragraph above them.
