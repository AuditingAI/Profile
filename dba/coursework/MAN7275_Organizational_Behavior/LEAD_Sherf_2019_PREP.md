## What the abstract establishes (verified)
- **Puzzle:** managers know fair treatment pays off, yet they still act unfairly. Why?
- **Shift:** earlier work is mostly "actor-centric" (the manager's traits). This paper moves the cause to the
  **context**: workload and rewards.
- **Theory:** the multiple-task pursuit framework. Under high workload, acting justly **competes** with other
  managerial tasks.
- **Signal:** organisations signal, implicitly and explicitly, that justice matters less than technical tasks.
- **Prediction:** high workload (amount of work plus time pressure) → managers prioritise technical tasks →
  **less justice rule adherence, unless justice is explicitly rewarded**.
- **Evidence:** three studies: an experience-sampling study, a field study and an experiment. Support across all three.
