# How Yasir works · read before any coursework reply (saved 14 Nov 2026 at his request)

## How he wants responses
1. **One step at a time.** Give the single next step, wait for "done", then give the next. Never a wall of steps.
2. **Lay out what's next.** Each reply ends with: the one step to do now, how long it takes, and where the finish line is.
3. **Answer first.** No preamble, no recap. Short.
4. **Ask with options.** When his input is needed, ask with clickable options, never an open question.
5. **Give an order, not a menu.** Recommend one path.

## House protocols (use these, not generic templates)
- **Slides:** the FIU house deck style in `dba/coursework/_templates/`. Examples: `build_w5_deck.py`,
  `build_project_deck_pptx.py`, `build_man7275_sherf_deck.py`. The rules:
  - FIU Blue `#081E3F` and FIU Gold `#B6862C`, Times type, and the FIU seal lockup on every slide.
  - Name, cohort and course on every slide.
  - **Every slide carries a figure. No slide is a list of sentences.**
  - Editable PowerPoint via `pptx_shim.py`, with the speaker script in the notes. Export a PDF of the same deck.
- **Documents:** `dba/coursework/_templates/build_dba_doc.py`. Times New Roman 11pt, the FIU seal masthead, no
  repository URL and no consulting brand on class submissions.
- **AI disclosure:** one short "Use of AI" line at the end, per FIU policy. Never more prominent than that.
- **Save to:** the GitHub repo (Scholar lane, his own paths) plus the FIU OneDrive working folder
  `FIU/TODO/<COURSE>/WORKING`.
