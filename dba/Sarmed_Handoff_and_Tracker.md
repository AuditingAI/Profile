## 📋 Study spec (paste into any panel quote request)

- **N size:** 100 valid completes (ask each panel how many to oversample for screen-outs)
- **Audience:** U.S.-based audit professionals — current or within last 24 months in **external audit, internal audit, or audit support**; experience on ≥1 continuing (multi-year) engagement; bachelor's+; English-fluent
- **Survey length:** ~15–20 min (anonymous, single session, ~73 items)
- **Quotas:** none required; a mix of external/internal auditors preferred if feasible
- **Other:** academic dissertation research (FIU DBA), IRB-25-0462; anonymous, on FIU Qualtrics; no download/video; not longitudinal/dyadic; has screeners + attention checks
- **Budget:** request a quote first (specialized auditor panels can run $20–$50+/complete)
