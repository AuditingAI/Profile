# Results — captures from runbook runs

**Append-only.** One file per run: `YYYY-MM-DD_RBnn.md`. Never overwrite a prior capture; a
superseded row is marked superseded, with a pointer to what replaced it.

## Why append-only

Two reasons, both methodological rather than tidiness.

**It makes drift visible.** If RB03 reports a rule as `proposed` in September and `in force` in
November, the pair of files is the evidence of the change. Overwriting destroys the only record that
the state moved.

**It is the audit trail for the research process itself.** The programme argues that AI-assisted work
carried forward without provenance thins the evidentiary basis of a judgment — that is L3. A research
pipeline whose own outputs are overwritten in place, with no record of where a claim entered, would
be an instance of the thing being argued against. Dated, append-only captures mean any row in a
chapter can be traced to the run that produced it, the query that found it, and the date.
