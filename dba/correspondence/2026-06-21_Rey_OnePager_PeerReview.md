**What the review changed (all wording, coverage, or controls — no construct redefined):**

- **Respondent-facing items: 71 → 73.**

**Reasoned decisions I am flagging for you:**

1. **5-point vs 7-point Likert.** The reviewer asked about 7-point. I recommend **keeping 5-point** — the measurement literature shows reliability/validity gains plateau by ~7 points and that 5- and 7-point scales perform comparably (Preston & Colman, 2000, *Acta Psychologica*; Dawes, 2008, *Int. J. Market Research*), while 5-point reduces fatigue across 55 items and renders better on mobile. **This is reversible before launch if you prefer 7-point** — please let me know.
2. **Regulatory + Professional Guidance as one construct.** The reviewer asked whether to split it. I am keeping it combined (both operate through the same accountability/System-2 mechanism), but the **EFA will test its dimensionality empirically** — if the items split into two factors, I will report it and revise the model with your sign-off.

**Scope and IRB.** Every change is wording, coverage, or a control; **none alters what a construct measures**, so none is a substantive IRB change. Monday's item-by-item reconciliation against the IRB-approved instrument will confirm this; anything judged substantive halts and escalates to you and the IRB before fielding.
