## 2. The bridge
- **Audit (done):** reviewers anchor on the prior year and, with AI, on the tool's first answer.
- **Mathematics (new collaboration):** experts check AI-produced proofs; mathematics has a formal checker, audit does
  not. A clean contrast case for when human review of AI output holds up.
- **Tax (next study, in design with faculty):** tax professionals and graduate tax students estimate the gains
  from moving a national tax administration's services onto AI, before and after seeing AI models' estimates.
  The shift toward the AI's numbers is anchoring; a promise of income support tests motivated reasoning.

## 3. What carries over directly
1. **Anchoring measure:** the qualifying study's anchoring logic becomes a before-and-after estimate (weight of advice).
2. **Feasibility first:** the AIB-LAC framework sizes the reachable pool of tax professionals before recruitment.
3. **Content analysis:** the GEB 7915 coding method analyses the written reasoning.
4. **Moderators:** workload and rewards (MAN 7275) become stake in the outcome and income support.
5. **AI-use governance:** prompts and AI outputs saved verbatim; disclosed under FIU policy; committee approves.
