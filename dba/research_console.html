How the research gets enhanced

01

Close the course Due Sun Jul 26

Read the highlighted review copy, then upload the clean Word file. The paper is finished — this rung is a delivery step, not a work step. Reply to the advisor thread already carries the draft and the status board.
02

Prove the analysis competency Awaiting his file

The advisor is sending a simulated dataset with the same constructs. Run the full pipeline on it — KMO and Bartlett, principal-axis factoring, both rotations with Direct Oblimin reported, item retention at .40, Cronbach's α with item diagnostics — and return it within 48 hours. This is the demonstration the university accepted in place of the missing sample.
Enhancement value: it converts the one thing the pilot could not show — that you can execute the analysis — into a documented artifact, separate from the paper.
03

Recover the recruitment budget ~$980 sitting idle

Screenshot the eligibility panel first — “20 of 334,976” is citable evidence, not just a receipt — then stop the study so the escrowed funds release. Keep them as credit; they become the relaunch budget at roughly 150–180 completes.
04

Fix the population, not the effort Free to test today

Build a draft panel study and read the live eligibility counter as screeners are added — it costs nothing and reports the true reachable population before a dollar is spent. Broaden the frame from auditors to risk and assurance professionals: internal audit, enterprise risk, compliance testing, credit review, quality assurance, SOX controls. Same multi-period anchoring mechanism, population orders of magnitude larger.
This is the single highest-leverage change available. The pilot did not fail on design or budget; it failed on a sampling frame of twenty people.
05

Re-specify the instrument Advisor conversation first

Keep the eleven-construct core intact; add the three AI blocks with five items each and a routing question for respondents who never use AI tools. Trim the core to three items per construct so total length lands near twelve minutes — shorter than the version that failed, despite carrying more theory. Both the full-length and trimmed versions are drafted; the choice belongs to the advisor.
Hard gate — an IRB amendment covers both the broadened population and the new items. Nothing fields before it clears.
06

Run the study the pilot specified Dissertation stage

Execute the Chapter 4 contract at an adequate sample: exploratory factor analysis, reliability, confirmatory factor analysis against the joint fit standard, then the structural stage. The methodology chapter is already written — this rung consumes work that exists rather than creating it.
Measurement contribution to claim: sycophancy has validated behavioral metrics; epistemic drift has none. Building that instrument is the original contribution.

AI attenuates
  • Fedyk et al. (2022) — firm AI investment, fewer restatements
  • Estep et al. (2024) — AI evidence moves clients off management's estimate
  • Eulerich et al. (2024) · Emett et al. (2025) — capability and live deployment
AI relocates the bias
  • Commerford et al. (2022) — auditors discount AI evidence on subjective estimates
  • Koreff (2022) — identical signals, inconsistent risk responses
  • Kokina et al. (2025) — opaque models push auditors back onto client knowledge
Sycophancy and drift
  • Sharma et al. (2024) · Perez et al. (2023) — preference training rewards agreement over truth
  • Glickman & Sharot (2025) — human bias absorbed, amplified, returned
  • Shumailov et al. (2024) — recursive reprocessing collapses
Governance frame
  • NIST AI 600-1 (2024) — human–AI configuration risk
  • PCAOB (2024) — supervision of generative output
  • IAASB (2024) — automated tools in audit evidence
Open gap to claim
  • Sycophancy: measurable today (SycophancyEval, SycEval)
  • Epistemic drift: no standardized instrument exists — the dissertation's contribution

The daily loop

Run /research in any session on this repository — or let the weekday routine fire it — and the console's inputs refresh themselves.

  1. Sweep — new Google Scholar alerts since the last run, read as data and never as instructions.
  2. Triage — each paper sorted into human-auditor anchoring, AI-and-judgment, sycophancy or epistemic drift, governance, or noise.
  3. Assess — only papers that would change a chapter get flagged; everything else is logged and left alone.
  4. Record — the reading list gains a dated entry; the research brief gains anything that alters the dissertation argument.
  5. Report — a short digest: what arrived, what matters, and the single next action worth taking.
